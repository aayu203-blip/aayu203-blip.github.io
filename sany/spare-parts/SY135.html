What It Is

The four-point contact slewing ring that allows the upper structure of the SY135/SY155 to rotate on the undercarriage. Internal gear ring drives the swing motor pinion. Bearing failure causes upper structure wobble and metal contamination of swing gear oil.

Why Parts Trading Company

Sany swing bearings — planned at 6,000-hour major overhaul. Mumbai stock. Export to Middle East and Africa common.

Symptoms of Failure

  • Upper structure wobble
  • Grinding swing noise
  • Metal in swing gear oil
  • Measurable play in upper structure.
