Installation & Application Notes

Never run an engine without a filter — even briefly causes abrasive damage. Pre-fill fuel filters with clean fuel before fitting to reduce prime time. Replace O-rings on filter housings every second service. Record change dates on filter housing.

Always consult the OEM service manual for model-specific torque values and procedures. WhatsApp our technical team if you need fitment guidance.

Failure Symptoms

A dirty air filter will cause a gradual reduction in engine power and responsiveness due to restricted airflow. If not replaced, this condition worsens, leading to inefficient fuel combustion, increased emissions, and potential engine strain. Continued operation with a severely blocked filter can lead to premature engine wear and reduced operational lifespan.

Installation Tip

Always clean the air filter housing thoroughly before installing the new filter to prevent any dirt from entering the engine. Ensure the new filter is properly seated and the seals are making good contact to prevent air bypass.

Service Note

When replacing the air filter, inspect the intake hoses and clamps for any signs of cracking or damage that could lead to air leaks.
