Installation & Application Notes

Never run an engine without a filter — even briefly causes abrasive damage. Pre-fill fuel filters with clean fuel before fitting to reduce prime time. Replace O-rings on filter housings every second service. Record change dates on filter housing.

Always consult the OEM service manual for model-specific torque values and procedures. WhatsApp our technical team if you need fitment guidance.

Failure Symptoms

A restricted air filter element will first cause a noticeable reduction in engine power and throttle response. If the restriction worsens, fuel efficiency will decline, and the engine may begin to run rough or produce excessive exhaust smoke. Continued operation with a severely clogged filter can lead to increased internal engine wear and potential damage.

Installation Tip

Before installing the new filter element, ensure the air cleaner housing is completely clean and free of debris. Make sure the seal on the new element seats properly to prevent air bypass.

Service Note

Inspect the air intake piping for cracks or leaks and clean the air cleaner housing thoroughly when replacing this filter element.
