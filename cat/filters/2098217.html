Installation & Application Notes

Never run an engine without a filter — even briefly causes abrasive damage. Pre-fill fuel filters with clean fuel before fitting to reduce prime time. Replace O-rings on filter housings every second service. Record change dates on filter housing.

Failure Symptoms

Initial signs of a failing filter component might include minor fluid leaks around the housing or a slight reduction in system pressure. If left unaddressed, the component's integrity can be compromised, leading to significant leaks, bypass of unfiltered fluid, and potential damage to downstream components. This can result in costly repairs and extended equipment downtime.

Installation Tip

Ensure the mounting surface is perfectly clean and flat before installing the new filter component to guarantee a leak-free seal. Do not overtighten mounting bolts, as this can warp the component or strip the threads.

Service Note

When replacing this filter component, it is crucial to inspect the filter element it houses for signs of premature failure or contamination, and check the condition of the fluid.
