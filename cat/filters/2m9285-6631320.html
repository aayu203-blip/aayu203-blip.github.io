Installation & Application Notes

Never run an engine without a filter — even briefly causes abrasive damage. Pre-fill fuel filters with clean fuel before fitting to reduce prime time. Replace O-rings on filter housings every second service. Record change dates on filter housing.

Failure Symptoms

A clogged air filter may initially cause a slight reduction in engine power or a noticeable increase in exhaust soot. If ignored, the engine will struggle to draw sufficient air, leading to poor fuel combustion, increased fuel consumption, and potential overheating. Continued operation with a severely restricted air filter can cause premature engine wear and damage.

Installation Tip

Ensure the air filter housing is completely clean inside before installing the new filter to prevent any dirt from bypassing the filter. Make sure the filter is seated correctly and the housing is sealed tightly to prevent unfiltered air entry.

Service Note

Inspect the intake manifold and turbocharger for any signs of oil or soot buildup when replacing the air filter.
