Installation & Application Notes

Never run an engine without a filter — even briefly causes abrasive damage. Pre-fill fuel filters with clean fuel before fitting to reduce prime time. Replace O-rings on filter housings every second service. Record change dates on filter housing.

Always consult the OEM service manual for model-specific torque values and procedures. WhatsApp our technical team if you need fitment guidance.

Failure Symptoms

Initial symptoms might include minor fluctuations in system pressure or slightly reduced performance. If the component fails or becomes clogged, it can lead to increased wear on other parts within the filtration system or the main system being filtered.

Installation Tip

Ensure all sealing surfaces are clean and free from debris before installing this component. Improper seating can lead to leaks or bypass of unfiltered fluid.

Service Note

Inspect the entire filter assembly, including housing and other elements, for wear or damage when replacing this component.
