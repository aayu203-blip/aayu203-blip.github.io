Failure Symptoms

Failure of these ground engaging tools initially presents as reduced digging efficiency and the loader struggling to penetrate tough material. Continued operation with worn teeth will lead to increased tire slippage and slower bucket loading times. The bucket's base metal can be significantly damaged if teeth are not replaced in a timely manner.

Installation Tip

Ensure the locking pin is fully seated and secured to prevent the tooth from dislodging during operation. Never attempt to install a tooth on a damaged or excessively worn adapter.

Service Note

Inspect the bucket adapter for cracks or excessive wear, and check the locking pin and retainer for damage during tooth replacement.
