Failure Symptoms

A failing bolt may initially manifest as a loose component or a rattling noise during operation. If the bolt continues to loosen or fail, the attached component can become detached, leading to reduced equipment performance and potential damage to other systems. The worst consequence is the uncontrolled detachment of a critical component, posing a significant risk to the equipment and operator.

Installation Tip

Always clean the bolt threads and the corresponding tapped hole or nut to ensure smooth engagement and accurate torque readings. Never reuse a bolt that shows signs of stretching, corrosion, or thread damage, as its clamping force will be compromised.

Service Note

When replacing bolts on ground engaging tools, inspect the condition of the mating components, such as the bucket adapter or wear plates, for any signs of wear or damage.
