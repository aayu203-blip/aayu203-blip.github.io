Failure Symptoms

Early signs of failure include a significant reduction in ripping depth and increased track slippage, especially in hard material. As the tip wears, its ability to break up the ground diminishes, leading to higher fuel consumption and strain on the tractor's powertrain. Continued operation with a worn ripper tip can cause severe damage to the ripper shank and potentially the tractor's rear frame.

Installation Tip

Ensure the ripper shank is clean and free of debris before mounting the new tip to guarantee a proper fit. Do not use excessive force or heat, as this can compromise the integrity of the tip or shank.

Service Note

Inspect the ripper shank for cracks or excessive wear during the replacement of the ripper tip.
