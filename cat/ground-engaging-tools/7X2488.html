Failure Symptoms

The initial symptom of a failing bolt is often a slight wobble or looseness in the attached ground engaging tool. If the bolt continues to degrade, it may shear, causing the tool to detach during operation. This can lead to uneven wear on other tools and potential damage to the bucket structure.

Installation Tip

Always use a new bolt and nut when replacing a worn or damaged one to ensure optimal clamping force. Ensure the bolt threads engage fully and are not cross-threaded during installation.

Service Note

Inspect the corresponding nut and the threaded holes in the adapter and bucket for any signs of wear or damage.
