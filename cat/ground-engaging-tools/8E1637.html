Failure Symptoms

Early signs of wear include a reduction in the loader's ability to effectively penetrate and fill the bucket. If left unaddressed, the tool will wear down, potentially damaging the bucket lip and reducing overall digging performance. Continued operation with a severely worn tool can lead to accelerated wear on the bucket structure and decreased machine efficiency.

Installation Tip

Ensure the bucket lip is completely clean and free of debris before mounting the new tool to guarantee proper seating. Avoid reusing old or damaged hardware, as it can compromise the security of the installation.

Service Note

Inspect the loader bucket lip, any adapters, and all mounting hardware for wear or damage during the replacement of this ground engaging tool.
