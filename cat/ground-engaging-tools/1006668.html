Failure Symptoms

A worn or damaged cutting edge will first manifest as reduced digging efficiency and increased tire slippage. If not replaced, the wear will extend to the bucket base, leading to more extensive and costly repairs. The worst outcome is severe damage to the bucket structure, rendering it unusable.

Installation Tip

Thoroughly clean the bucket lip and mounting surfaces to ensure a flush fit and prevent premature wear. Do not reuse worn or damaged mounting bolts; always use new hardware.

Service Note

Inspect bucket teeth and adapters for wear and damage when replacing the cutting edge.
