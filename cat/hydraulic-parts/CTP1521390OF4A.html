Failure Symptoms

Early indicators of a failing hydraulic pump include a noticeable reduction in the speed and power of hydraulic functions. If ignored, the pump may start to make excessive noise, and hydraulic performance will continue to degrade, potentially leading to overheating of the hydraulic oil. Continued operation risks damage to other hydraulic components due to insufficient flow or contamination.

Installation Tip

Thoroughly clean the hydraulic system, including the tank and lines, before installing the new pump to prevent immediate contamination. Never run the pump dry; ensure it is properly primed with hydraulic fluid before initial startup.

Service Note

Replace the main hydraulic filter and inspect the hydraulic fluid for wear particles when replacing the hydraulic pump.
