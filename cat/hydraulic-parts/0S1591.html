Failure Symptoms

Visible hydraulic fluid seepage around the connection point is the first sign of a failing cap screw seal. If ignored, the leak may worsen, leading to a gradual loss of hydraulic pressure and system performance. Continued operation with a significant leak can result in insufficient hydraulic function and potential damage to other system components.

Installation Tip

Ensure the mating surfaces and threads are clean and free of debris before installation to achieve a proper seal. Overtightening this fastener can strip the threads or damage the component it secures.

Service Note

Inspect the sealing washer or O-ring associated with this cap screw for signs of wear or damage and replace if necessary.
