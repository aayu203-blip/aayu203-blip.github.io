Failure Symptoms

Early signs of a failing hydraulic pump cartridge include a noticeable decrease in the speed and force of hydraulic functions. If ignored, the pump may begin to make irregular noises and internal leakage will increase, leading to overheating and reduced system efficiency. Continued operation risks significant damage to the pump and other hydraulic components.

Installation Tip

Carefully inspect the pump housing for any scoring or wear marks before installing the new cartridge, as these can compromise the seal and performance. Ensure all internal components are properly seated and aligned during assembly.

Service Note

When replacing the pump cartridge, it is crucial to inspect the hydraulic pump housing for wear and replace the hydraulic filter and fluid.
