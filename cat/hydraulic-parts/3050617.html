Failure Symptoms

Early signs of failure might include a slight sluggishness in hydraulic functions or a subtle increase in hydraulic fluid temperature. If left unaddressed, the component's performance will degrade, leading to significantly reduced hydraulic power and potential system instability. Continued operation risks damage to other hydraulic system components.

Installation Tip

Ensure the hydraulic system is completely clean and free of any contaminants before installing the new component. Avoid overtightening any connections, as this can compromise seals and lead to leaks.

Service Note

Inspect the hydraulic fluid and filter for contamination, and check associated seals and hoses for wear during replacement.
