Failure Symptoms

A failing working light switch might initially fail to turn the lights on consistently, or the lights may flicker intermittently. If the internal contacts degrade or become damaged, the switch may cease to function entirely, leaving the operator without essential lighting. The risk is reduced visibility in low-light conditions, increasing the potential for accidents or operational errors.

Installation Tip

Ensure the equipment's battery is disconnected before attempting to replace the switch to prevent electrical shorts. Avoid forcing the switch into the dashboard opening; ensure it is the correct type and orientation.

Service Note

Inspect the wiring harness connected to the switch for any signs of damage, corrosion, or loose connections during replacement.
