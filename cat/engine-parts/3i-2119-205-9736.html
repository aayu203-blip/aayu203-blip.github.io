Failure Symptoms

A clogged air filter assembly will first manifest as a gradual loss of engine power and a noticeable increase in fuel consumption. If the restriction becomes severe, the engine may struggle to accelerate, and excessive black smoke may appear due to incomplete combustion. Continued operation with a severely restricted air filter can lead to premature engine wear and potential damage.

Installation Tip

Ensure the filter housing sealing surfaces are clean and free of debris before installing the new air filter assembly to guarantee a proper seal. Do not operate the equipment without the air filter installed, even for short periods.

Service Note

Inspect the turbocharger and intake manifold for any signs of oil contamination or carbon buildup when replacing the air filter assembly.
