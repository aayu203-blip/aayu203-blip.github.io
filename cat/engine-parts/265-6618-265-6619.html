Failure Symptoms

Reduced airflow from the cabin vents and a musty or dusty smell are the first signs that the cabin air filters need replacement. If ignored, the HVAC system's efficiency will continue to decline, and the cabin environment will become increasingly uncomfortable and potentially unhealthy due to airborne contaminants. This can lead to operator fatigue and reduced productivity.

Installation Tip

Before installing the new filters, ensure the filter housing is clean and free of debris, as this is where contaminants can accumulate. Pay close attention to the airflow direction arrows on the filters to ensure correct installation.

Service Note

While replacing the cabin air filters, inspect the HVAC system's blower motor and air intake vents for any obstructions or excessive dirt buildup.
