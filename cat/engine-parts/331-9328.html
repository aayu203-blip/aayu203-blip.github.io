Installation & Application Notes

Inspect mating surfaces for wear before fitting. Use torque specs from service manual. Replace gaskets and seals as a set — reusing compressed gaskets causes oil leaks. Clean all oil passages with compressed air.

Failure Symptoms

The first indication of seal failure is often visible hydraulic fluid leakage from the cylinder rod seal or around the cylinder body. If not addressed, the leakage will increase, leading to slower and less responsive bucket operation. The ultimate consequence is a significant loss of hydraulic system pressure, rendering the bucket unusable and potentially causing damage to other hydraulic components.

Installation Tip

Thoroughly clean all internal surfaces of the cylinder bore and piston before installing the new seals to prevent damage. Ensure all seals are properly seated and lubricated before reassembly to avoid tearing.

Service Note

When rebuilding a hydraulic cylinder, inspect the cylinder barrel for scoring and the piston rod for straightness and damage.
