Failure Symptoms

A failing solenoid valve may initially cause intermittent issues, such as hesitation or a slight loss of power. If the problem persists, the valve may become completely unresponsive, leading to a significant reduction in performance or the inability to operate certain engine functions. Continued operation with a faulty solenoid can result in inefficient fuel consumption and potential damage to related engine components.

Installation Tip

Always disconnect the battery before working on electrical components like solenoid valves. Ensure the electrical connector is properly seated and secured after installation to prevent intermittent connections.

Service Note

When replacing a solenoid valve, it is advisable to check and clean any associated filters or screens in the fluid circuit it controls.
