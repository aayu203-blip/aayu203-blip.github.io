Installation & Application Notes

Inspect mating surfaces for wear before fitting. Use torque specs from service manual. Replace gaskets and seals as a set — reusing compressed gaskets causes oil leaks. Clean all oil passages with compressed air.

Always consult the OEM service manual for model-specific torque values and procedures. WhatsApp our technical team if you need fitment guidance.

Failure Symptoms

Early indications of a failing two-section gear pump include a noticeable whining or groaning noise from the pump housing and a decrease in the speed or responsiveness of hydraulic functions. If left unaddressed, the pump's output will continue to diminish, potentially leading to overheating of the hydraulic oil and increased wear on other hydraulic components. Continued operation risks hydraulic system failure, preventing equipment operation.

Installation Tip

Thoroughly clean the pump mounting surface and ensure all old gasket material is removed to achieve a proper seal. Never reuse old hydraulic fittings or hoses; replace them if there is any sign of damage or wear.

Service Note

Always inspect the hydraulic system for contamination and replace the hydraulic filter element when servicing or replacing the gear pump.
