This is a 24-volt fuel transfer pump designed to supply fuel from the tank to the injection system. It operates under electrical power to ensure consistent fuel pressure and flow, preventing fuel starvation during engine operation. A failing pump may cause intermittent engine sputtering, difficulty starting, or a complete loss of power.

Installation & Application Notes

Use clean, filtered fuel — fuel injection components have micron-level tolerances. Prime fuel system before cranking to avoid dry-start damage. Replace fuel filter whenever servicing injection components. Calibrate injectors after replacement if equipment has ECU.

Failure Symptoms

Early signs of a failing fuel transfer pump include occasional engine hesitation or stumbling, especially under load. If left unaddressed, the engine may become difficult to start, run rough, or shut down unexpectedly due to insufficient fuel supply. Continued operation risks engine damage from fuel starvation and inconsistent combustion.

Installation Tip

Ensure the fuel tank is at least half full to prevent the pump from running dry during priming and initial operation. Do not reuse old fuel lines or clamps, as they can degrade and cause leaks.

Service Note

Inspect the fuel filter and fuel lines for any signs of clogging or damage when replacing the fuel transfer pump.
