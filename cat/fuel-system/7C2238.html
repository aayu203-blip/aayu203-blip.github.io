Installation & Application Notes

Use clean, filtered fuel — fuel injection components have micron-level tolerances. Prime fuel system before cranking to avoid dry-start damage. Replace fuel filter whenever servicing injection components. Calibrate injectors after replacement if equipment has ECU.

Failure Symptoms

A failing nozzle may initially cause uneven engine running or a slight increase in exhaust smoke. If ignored, the engine may experience significant power loss, misfires, and increased fuel consumption. Continued operation risks washing lubricating oil from cylinder walls, leading to accelerated internal engine wear.

Installation Tip

Ensure the nozzle seat area in the cylinder head is perfectly clean before installation to prevent leaks. Do not reuse old copper washers or seals; always use new ones.

Service Note

When replacing fuel nozzles, it is recommended to inspect and replace the fuel filter and check the condition of the fuel lines.
