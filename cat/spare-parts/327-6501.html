Failure Symptoms

A failing fuel filter can initially cause a slight hesitation during acceleration or a minor reduction in engine power. As it becomes more restricted, the engine may start to misfire, run rough, or become difficult to start, especially under load. If the filter becomes completely blocked, the engine will stall and be unable to restart.

Installation Tip

Prime the new filter with clean fuel before installation to minimize air in the system and reduce cranking time. Ensure the O-ring seal is properly seated and lubricated to prevent fuel leaks.

Service Note

Inspect the fuel tank for sediment and check the fuel lines for any signs of wear or damage when replacing the fuel filter.
