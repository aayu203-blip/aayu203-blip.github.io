Failure Symptoms

Imprecise or jerky movements of loader functions are the first indicators of a failing joystick. If left unaddressed, control inputs may become significantly delayed or completely unresponsive. This can lead to inefficient operation and potential difficulty in precise maneuvering.

Installation Tip

Carefully disconnect the electrical connectors, noting their positions, before removing the old joystick. Ensure the new joystick is seated correctly in its mounting before reconnecting the wiring.

Service Note

Inspect the hydraulic control valve and associated linkages for any signs of wear or leakage when replacing the joystick handle.
