Installation & Application Notes

Flush cooling system with distilled water before refilling. Use pre-mixed coolant — never plain water, which causes corrosion. Pressure-test system after any cooling repair. Check thermostat by immersing in hot water — should open at rated temperature.

Failure Symptoms

The first indication of a failing blower motor is usually a decrease in airflow from the cabin vents or intermittent operation. If ignored, the motor may cease to function entirely, or it could begin to make loud grinding or squealing noises. This leaves the operator without essential climate control, impacting comfort and visibility.

Installation Tip

Ensure the cabin air filter is clean and not obstructing airflow before installing the new blower motor. Avoid forcing the motor into its housing, as this can damage the mounting tabs or the fan wheel.

Service Note

Inspect the cabin air filter and the HVAC system ducting for obstructions or damage when replacing the blower fan motor.
