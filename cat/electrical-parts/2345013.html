Failure Symptoms

The first sign of a failing temperature sensor is typically an inaccurate temperature reading on the equipment's display or in diagnostic data. This can lead to the engine or hydraulic system operating outside its optimal temperature range, potentially causing reduced performance or increased wear. If left unaddressed, it could result in overheating or component damage due to improper thermal management.

Installation Tip

Ensure the threaded portion of the sensor is clean and free of debris before installation to guarantee a proper seal. Do not overtighten the sensor, as this can damage the threads in the component it screws into.

Service Note

When replacing a temperature sensor, it is advisable to check the coolant or hydraulic fluid level and condition, and inspect the radiator or heat exchanger for blockages.
