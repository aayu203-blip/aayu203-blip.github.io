Installation & Application Notes

Disconnect main battery before working on electrical components. Use dielectric grease on all connector pins. Check ground connections — most electrical faults trace to poor grounds. Test with multimeter before installing new parts.

Failure Symptoms

The first indication of a failing relay may be a clicking sound without the associated circuit activating, or a complete lack of response from the controlled system. If ignored, the relay can become stuck open or closed, leading to continuous operation or complete inoperability of the affected component. This can result in the starter motor being engaged unnecessarily or critical systems remaining powered, potentially draining the battery.

Installation Tip

Before installation, carefully compare the terminal layout of the new relay with the old one to ensure correct placement. Avoid forcing the relay into its socket, as this can damage the terminals.

Service Note

When replacing this relay, inspect the associated wiring and fuses for any signs of overheating or corrosion.
