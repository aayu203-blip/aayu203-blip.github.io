Installation & Application Notes

Disconnect main battery before working on electrical components. Use dielectric grease on all connector pins. Check ground connections — most electrical faults trace to poor grounds. Test with multimeter before installing new parts.

Always consult the OEM service manual for model-specific torque values and procedures. WhatsApp our technical team if you need fitment guidance.

Failure Symptoms

The first sign of a failing battery terminal is often a weak or intermittent electrical connection, leading to slow engine cranking. If ignored, this can progress to complete loss of power to the starter and other electrical components. This could result in the inability to start the equipment.

Installation Tip

Clean both the battery post and the inside of the terminal thoroughly before installation to ensure optimal conductivity. Do not overtighten the terminal clamp bolt, as this can strip the threads or crack the terminal.

Service Note

Inspect the battery cables for fraying or corrosion and clean the battery tray area when replacing the terminal.
