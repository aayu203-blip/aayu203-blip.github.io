Failure Symptoms

An initial symptom of a failing temperature switch might be an inaccurate temperature gauge reading or a warning light that is constantly on or off. If ignored, the machine's control system may not receive correct temperature data, leading to improper engine performance or cooling system operation. This can result in potential engine damage due to overheating or inefficient operation.

Installation Tip

Ensure the engine or system is cool before attempting to remove the old switch to avoid burns or fluid spillage. Apply a small amount of thread sealant to the threads of the new switch, but avoid getting sealant into the switch's sensing area.

Service Note

Inspect the coolant level and condition, and check the radiator and fan for obstructions or damage.
