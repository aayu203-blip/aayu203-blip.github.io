Installation & Application Notes

Disconnect main battery before working on electrical components. Use dielectric grease on all connector pins. Check ground connections — most electrical faults trace to poor grounds. Test with multimeter before installing new parts.

Always consult the OEM service manual for model-specific torque values and procedures. WhatsApp our technical team if you need fitment guidance.

Failure Symptoms

Loose or missing fasteners securing the bin, leading to exposed wiring, is an initial indicator of a problem. If ignored, the exposed components are vulnerable to moisture, dust, and physical damage, increasing the risk of electrical shorts. This can result in unpredictable machine operation and potential downtime.

Installation Tip

Ensure all mounting points are free of debris and corrosion before attaching the new bin to ensure a secure fit. Do not use excessive force when securing fasteners, as this can damage the bin or mounting points.

Service Note

Inspect the wiring and connectors housed within the bin for any signs of wear, damage, or corrosion during replacement.
