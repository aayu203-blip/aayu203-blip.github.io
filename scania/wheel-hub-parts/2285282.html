What It Is

The pressed-in bolt stud that passes through the brake drum or disc hat and wheel, retained by the wheel nut. Scania uses a specific stud material and press-fit knurl specification — OEM replacements only to maintain correct retention force.

Why Parts Trading Company

Always replace wheel bolts in sets — fatigue crack propagation means one failed stud indicates adjacent studs are near end of life. Pack of 10 available. Mumbai.

Symptoms of Failure

  • Wheel pull-out on stripped stud
  • Loose wheel with correct torque (failed press fit)
  • Thread strip
  • Fatigue fracture at stud base.
