About Scania Truck Bearings

Bearings are among the highest-wear components in any truck drivetrain. In Scania trucks, bearings operate under sustained high load, heat, and — in mining and construction applications — significant contamination exposure. Running a bearing past its service life is one of the most common causes of catastrophic axle and gearbox failures.

When to replace: Signs of bearing failure include a persistent humming or rumbling noise that changes with speed, abnormal heat at a hub or axle, vibration under load, and visible grease leakage from the bearing housing. Many fleet operators replace bearings preventively at major overhaul intervals rather than waiting for failure.
