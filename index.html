Frequently Asked Questions

What brands of heavy equipment parts does Parts Trading Company supply?

Parts Trading Company supplies OEM-specification and aftermarket parts for Volvo, Scania, Komatsu, Caterpillar (CAT), Hitachi, Kobelco, JCB, Doosan, Liebherr, Atlas Copco, Epiroc, Sandvik, Wirtgen, and 10+ additional brands. Our Mumbai warehouse stocks 70,000+ catalogued parts for same-day dispatch.

How quickly can Parts Trading Company dispatch parts from Mumbai?

Orders confirmed before 3 PM IST (Monday–Saturday) are dispatched the same day from our Grant Road, Mumbai warehouse. Most Indian destinations receive delivery within 2–4 business days. International orders ship by air freight with typical transit of 3–12 days depending on destination.

Does Parts Trading Company export heavy equipment parts internationally?

Yes. Parts Trading Company has exported to 50+ countries since 1956, including UAE, Russia, Indonesia, South Africa, Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana, Egypt, Australia, and across Southeast Asia. All international orders include commercial invoice, packing list, and certificate of origin. Payment via SWIFT wire transfer, UPI, or bank transfer.

Are the parts OEM or aftermarket?

Parts Trading Company supplies both OEM-specification aftermarket parts (manufactured to original engineering drawings) and genuine OEM parts. All aftermarket parts are new units — not remanufactured. We source from certified component manufacturers including DAYCO, FAG, INA, WABCO, VALEO, FERODO, LUK, DENSO, MAHLE, and Garrett.
