## 4. Universal nav + footer stamped to all pages (`stamp_chrome.py`)

**Problem:** 4 different navs and 3 different footers existed across 93K+ pages.
- Blog posts had hard-coded "Komatsu Parts" nav link regardless of topic
- Product pages had `console.log` debug code in nav (live in ~51K pages)
- About page had `h-36` logo (3.6× taller than other pages)
- Footer disclaimer, GST number, and business hours inconsistently present
- Copyright showed "© 2025" on brand hub pages

**Solution:** Created `stamp_chrome.py` — a Python script that stamps canonical nav + footer onto every website HTML file.

**Canonical nav:**
- White sticky bar, `h-16/h-20` responsive height
- Logo at `h-10/h-12` — consistent across all pages
- 6 links: Home · Models · Products · Brands · Blog · About
- Green WhatsApp CTA button
- Alpine.js mobile dropdown
- Automatically injected into pages that were missing Alpine.js

**Canonical footer:**
- 3-column dark grid
- Col 1: Logo + Est. 1956 + address + GST: 27AAAFP1087E1ZG
- Col 2: Navigate links + Brand Parts hub shortcuts (Volvo, Scania, Komatsu, CAT, Hitachi, Kobelco)
- Col 3: Contact — WhatsApp (prominent green button), landline, mobile, email, hours Mon–Sat 9–6
- Social row: Instagram + LinkedIn + © 2026 + Privacy/Terms links
- **Disclaimer:** "All brand names, logos, images, and part numbers used on this website are for identification and reference purposes only. Parts Trading Company is not affiliated with any original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) unless specifically stated..."

**Scope:** 91,087 files changed. Homepage (`index.html`) excluded — keeps custom scroll-aware nav and full contact/maps footer.

**Script usage:**
```bash
python3 stamp_chrome.py          # dry run
python3 stamp_chrome.py --write  # apply
```
