About LiuGong

LiuGong (Guangxi LiuGong Machinery, founded 1958) is one of China's largest heavy equipment manufacturers, producing excavators, wheel loaders, backhoe loaders, motor graders, and road construction equipment. LiuGong CLG series excavators are widely used across India, Africa, and the Middle East.
