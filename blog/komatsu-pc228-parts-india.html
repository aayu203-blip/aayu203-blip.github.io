Komatsu PC228 — The Versatile Short-Tail Swing Excavator

The Komatsu PC228US is the short-tail-swing variant of the PC228 class — designed for road maintenance, urban excavation, and confined site work where conventional machine tail swing is a hazard to workers, passing traffic, or adjacent structures. In India, municipal road departments, NHAI contractors, and urban utility companies have adopted the PC228US as the machine that delivers near-20-tonne performance in the operating envelope of a smaller machine.

The PC228 series uses the SAA6D107E engine — the same engine family as the PC200-8, making parts cross-compatible across a significant portion of the filter and engine component range. This cross-compatibility is a significant advantage for fleet operators running both PC200 and PC228 machines.

PC228 Engine — SAA6D107E-1

The PC228US-8 uses the SAA6D107E-1 engine in 6.7-litre common-rail specification, producing approximately 118 kW (158 hp) — slightly higher output than the PC200-8 to compensate for the PC228's greater operating weight (22.8 tonnes). PC228US — Short Tail Swing Considerations

The US (Ultra Short) tail swing design places the counterweight within the track width when the upper structure rotates. This design requires attention to: (1) counterweight clearance when swinging near walls — the machine requires operating practice different from conventional excavators, and (2) counterweight attachment bolt torque — check at every 1,000-hour service as the counterweight is positioned differently from standard machines and the bolt stress pattern differs.

Many PC228US machines are used with hydraulic breakers, compactors, and sorting grabs in addition to the standard bucket. When using attachments, verify the auxiliary hydraulic flow and pressure settings match the attachment specification — incorrect settings cause premature attachment pump and motor failure.

Kawasaki K3V112DT Pump — PC228

The PC228US uses the Kawasaki K3V112DT pump — the same pump as the PC200-7. This means full cross-compatibility of pump rebuild components with the PC200-7 generation. Diagnosis and rebuild procedures are identical to the PC200 guide.
