The Situation

Peak production season in Jharkhand's coal belt means every hour of downtime translates directly into lost tonnage and contract penalties. When one of the region's established mining operators called PTC, they had three machines down simultaneously: a Komatsu PC800-8 excavator, a second PC800, and two Komatsu HD785-7 dump trucks.

The failures were not random. A batch of hydraulic fluid from an unvetted supplier had introduced particulate contamination across the fleet during a scheduled maintenance cycle. The result: hydraulic pumps, cylinder seal kits, and swing motor components across all three machines had failed or were failing.

Standard lead time through OEM channels: 3–4 weeks. The operator's mine plan allowed for zero weeks.

What PTC Was Asked to Supply

The operator's maintenance engineer sent PTC a consolidated requirement list via WhatsApp at 7:30 AM. The list ran to 47 line items across both machine types:

  • Komatsu PC800 hydraulic main pump assemblies
  • PC800 cylinder seal kits (boom, arm, bucket)
  • PC800 swing motor assembly components
  • PC800 hydraulic filter elements
  • HD785 hydraulic pump seal kits
  • HD785 hoist cylinder seal kits
  • HD785 steering cylinder seals
  • Miscellaneous O-ring and gasket sets

Several of these — particularly the PC800 main pump and swing motor components — are considered slow-moving items that most suppliers do not hold in stock.

How PTC Responded

PTC's team confirmed stock availability against all 47 line items by 10:15 AM — under three hours from the initial enquiry. A proforma invoice was issued the same morning. Payment was confirmed before noon.

All 47 parts were picked, packaged, and dispatched from PTC's Mumbai warehouse that afternoon via road freight to Dhanbad (Jharkhand). Given the volume and weight, PTC coordinated directly with the freight forwarder to ensure the consignment arrived as a single consolidated shipment — not in pieces across multiple days.

The parts arrived at the mine site on day two. The operator's engineering team began fitting immediately. All three machines were back in operation within 72 hours of the initial call.

Why This Matters for Mining Operations

Simultaneous failures — especially during peak season — are a procurement department's worst scenario. Most parts suppliers are set up for planned maintenance orders, not emergency multi-machine recoveries. The combination of factors that made this possible:

  • Deep Komatsu inventory: PTC maintains buffer stock on PC800 and HD785 components specifically because Indian coal mining fleets run large numbers of these machines.
  • Single source for all 47 items: No chasing multiple suppliers, no partial shipments, no coordination overhead for the operator.
  • Same-day dispatch: Parts confirmed, invoiced, picked, and moving on the same day as the order.
