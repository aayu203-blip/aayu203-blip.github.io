Kobelco SK330 — The 33-Tonne Excavator Built for Quarry Productivity

The Kobelco SK330 is the machine that serious quarry and large infrastructure contractors choose when they need Kobelco's hydraulic precision at 33-tonne operating weight. In India's stone quarries of Rajasthan, granite quarries of Andhra Pradesh, and limestone operations of Madhya Pradesh, the SK330 is found in fleets alongside Hitachi ZX330s and CAT 336s. Its CLSS hydraulic system and excellent fuel economy give it a running cost advantage that experienced operators appreciate over long campaigns.

The active generations in Indian fleets are SK330-8 (2008–2015) and SK330-10 (2015–present). The generation change brought a new engine and updated hydraulic control — parts ordering must be confirmed against the serial number.

SK330 Generation Guide

Kobelco SK330-8 (2008–2015) — Hino J08E Engine

The SK330-8 uses the Hino J08E-TA engine — an 8.0-litre six-cylinder turbocharged common-rail unit producing 184 kW (247 hp). The Hino J08E is one of the most reliable heavy-duty engines in the Japanese market and has good aftermarket coverage for filters, injectors, and overhaul components. The hydraulic pump is a Kawasaki K3V180 — a higher-displacement version of the K3V112 used in smaller Kobelco excavators, with the same fundamental architecture.

Kobelco SK330-10 (2015–Present) — Hino J08E-TF Engine

The SK330-10 updates the engine to the J08E-TF variant meeting Tier 4 Final / BS IV requirements, with revised turbocharger and aftertreatment system. Power increases to 194 kW (260 hp). The hydraulic system was updated with SK330-10 specific main valve block — parts for the main control valve differ from the -8 generation. Cab improvements include the 8-inch colour monitor and Kobelco's machine management system.

Kawasaki K3V180 Pump — SK330 Diagnostics

The K3V180 is a large-displacement version of the K3V family. Case drain flow measurement (normal: below 8 L/min per section at rated speed) is the primary diagnostic indicator. Elevated case drain flow combined with high hydraulic oil temperature indicates slipper pad or valve plate wear. Before condemning the pump, always verify pilot filter condition, pilot relief pressure (3.9 MPa), and hydraulic oil quality — dark, burnt-smelling oil accelerates pump wear dramatically and a pump rebuild with contaminated oil will fail quickly.

SK330 Undercarriage

The SK330 long-carriage undercarriage uses 50 links per side for most configurations. Bottom rollers: 9 per side (18 total). The higher operating weight versus the SK210 means proportionally faster undercarriage wear in equivalent rock conditions. SALT chain is strongly recommended for Indian quarry applications — the return on SALT investment at 33-tonne operating weight is typically achieved within the first undercarriage replacement cycle.
