Common Scania Brake Problems & Solutions

Diagnose low air pressure, slack adjuster failures, and uneven braking on Scania P/G/R series trucks.

The Scania Brake System: Robust but Complex

Scania trucks (P, G, R series) use a sophisticated pneumatic brake system. While reliable, the harsh conditions of mining and long-haul transport in India can cause specific issues.

1. Low Air Pressure Warnings

Symptoms: The buzzer doesn't stop, or the gauge stays in the red.

Fix: Check the APM (Air Processing Management) unit. Often, the desiccant cartridge is saturated with oil/water, or the unloader valve is stuck. We recommend replacing the dryer cartridge every 6 months.

2. Slack Adjuster Failure

Symptoms: Uneven braking or the truck pulls to one side.

Fix: Automatic Slack Adjusters (ASAs) can seize due to lack of grease. If manual adjustment doesn't hold, the internal clutch is stripped. Do not weld the adjuster arm; replace it immediately.

3. ABS Sensor Faults

Symptoms: ABS light on dashboard.

Fix: Usually debris on the tone ring or an incorrect air gap. Clean the sensor head and push it back until it touches the ring (it will self-adjust).
