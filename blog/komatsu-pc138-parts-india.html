Komatsu PC138 — The Versatile 14-Tonne Excavator

The Komatsu PC138 fills the gap between the small 8-tonne PC78 and the benchmark 20-tonne PC200. In India, it has found a strong market among utility contractors, municipal corporations, and road agencies who need more digging force than an 8-tonne machine but do not need — or cannot transport — a full 20-tonne excavator. The PC138US Zero Tail Swing variant is particularly popular in urban settings where conventional tail swing would cause clearance problems with passing traffic or adjacent structures.

The active generations are: PC138-8 (2008–2016) and PC138-11 (2016–present). Both use the SAA4D95LE engine — a 4-cylinder version of the 6D95 engine family that powered the PC200-6.

PC138 Engine — SAA4D95LE

The Komatsu SAA4D95LE is a 3.3-litre four-cylinder turbocharged engine producing approximately 70 kW (94 hp) in PC138-8 specification. Like the full-size 6D95, the 4D95 is mechanically injected on most specifications sold into India and developing markets — making it exceptionally field-serviceable. Engine parts including injectors, injection pump kits, water pumps, and thermostat are widely available through both Komatsu dealers and the global aftermarket for Komatsu 4D95 applications.

PC138 Hydraulic Pump — K3V63

The PC138 uses the Kawasaki K3V63 tandem axial-piston pump — the smaller sibling of the K3V112 used in the PC200. Rebuild components (cylinder block, pistons, valve plate) follow the same K3V architecture; specialist rebuilders who service K3V112 units can typically rebuild the K3V63 as well. Diagnosis: case drain flow above 4–5 L/min at rated speed indicates significant wear.

PC138US Zero Tail Swing — Special Considerations

The PC138US has the counterweight and upper structure redesigned to eliminate tail swing overhang. This enables it to work in trenches, alongside walls, and in urban settings without the conventional rear clearance requirement. The US variant has some unique upper structure parts (counterweight, engine cover, hydraulic lines) — confirm "US" on the serial plate when ordering these components. Engine and hydraulic pump parts are shared with the standard PC138.
