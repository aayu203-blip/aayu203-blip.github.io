OEM vs Aftermarket Parts: The Honest Truth

When should you buy Genuine, and when is Aftermarket safe? A candid guide for fleet owners.

The Great Debate

We sell both, so we can be honest. OEM (Genuine) parts are guaranteed to work, but Aftermarket parts can save you 40-60%. How do you choose?

When to Buy OEM

  • Sensors & ECUs: Aftermarket electronics often fail to communicate with the vehicle CAN bus.
  • Internal Engine Parts: Piston rings and bearings. The labor cost to re-open an engine is too high to risk it.

When to Buy Aftermarket

  • Body Panels: Bumpers, steps, mirrors. They break often and don't affect performance.
  • Filters: High-quality aftermarket filters (like Donaldson or Fleetguard) are often the same as OEM.
  • Suspension: Bushings and torque rods. Good aftermarket rubber is indistinguishable from Genuine.

The "Gray Market" Warning

Beware of "unboxed" Genuine parts. If it's not in a sealed Volvo/Scania box, treat it as aftermarket. At PTC, we clearly label everything.
