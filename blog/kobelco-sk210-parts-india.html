Kobelco SK200/SK210 — Japan's Hydraulic Precision Leader

Kobelco excavators occupy a distinct position in the Indian market. While Komatsu and Hitachi compete on breadth of features and brand volume, Kobelco has cultivated loyalty among operators who prize hydraulic precision and fuel efficiency above all else. The SK200 and SK210 are the most widely sold Kobelco models in India, Southeast Asia, and Sub-Saharan Africa — and they form the core of PTC's Kobelco parts inventory.

The naming can be confusing: Kobelco sold the SK200 and SK200-8 before transitioning to the SK210 designation from the SK200-10 generation onwards in some markets. For parts purposes, the engine and hydraulic architecture are what matters — and this guide covers both the SK200-8 and SK210-10 in detail.

SK200/SK210 Generation Guide

Kobelco SK200-8 (2008–2015) — Serial: YY02UXXXX

The SK200-8 uses the Isuzu AH-4HK1X engine — a 5.2-litre four-cylinder turbocharged common-rail unit producing 110 kW (148 hp). Unlike the Hitachi ZX200-3's mechanically injected 6BG1, the SK200-8's 4HK1 is a common-rail engine — meaning fuel system cleanliness is critical. The hydraulic pump is the Kawasaki K3V112DT, the same platform used in the Komatsu PC200-7 and -8, giving excellent aftermarket parts availability.

The SK200-8 introduced Kobelco's iNDr (Integrated No-heat Dust and Noise Reduction) cooling system, which separates the radiator from the engine cooling air path to reduce noise and prevent recirculation of hot exhaust air over the radiator. This system requires attention to the cooling partition seals during service — blocked or torn seals reduce cooling efficiency significantly.

Kobelco SK210-10 (2015–Present) — Serial: YQ02UXXXX and later

The SK210-10 uses the Hino J05E-TA engine — a 5.0-litre four-cylinder turbocharged common-rail unit producing 114 kW (153 hp). This is Kobelco's Tier 4 Final / Stage IV compliant engine. The hydraulic pump transitions to a Kawasaki K3V112DTP (updated port plate) and the hydraulic control system includes Kobelco's SELEMO (Selective Energy Loss Minimum) controller for fuel consumption optimisation. The 5-inch colour monitor provides machine-hour, fuel consumption, and warning management.

Common-rail caution: Both the 4HK1 (SK200-8) and J05E (SK210-10) are common-rail engines. Run only quality secondary fuel filters and change them at 500 hours. Contaminated fuel destroys injectors at ₹20,000–35,000 each. In India, consider halving filter change intervals if operating on substandard diesel.

Kawasaki K3V112 Hydraulic Pump — SK200 Diagnostics

The SK200-8's K3V112DT pump is the same platform found in the Komatsu PC200-8, meaning rebuild parts and exchange units sourced for either machine are typically cross-applicable (confirm casting numbers). Pump failure presents as slow attachment response, weak digging force, or hydraulic overheating. Before condemning the pump, verify: pilot filter condition, pilot relief pressure (3.9 MPa), and case drain flow (normal: below 6 L/min at rated speed).

Isuzu 4HK1 Engine — SK200-8 Common Issues

  • Injector failure from contaminated fuel — The 4HK1 common-rail system (160 MPa) is highly sensitive to fuel cleanliness. Use dual filtration and change secondary filters at 500-hour intervals.
  • EGR valve carbon fouling — The SK200-8's EGR system fouls quickly on Indian diesel. Clean EGR valve and cooler at 2,000-hour intervals.
  • Turbocharger oil coking — Allow the engine to idle 2–3 minutes before shutdown to cool the turbocharger bearing. Immediate engine-off after high load causes oil to coke on the turbo shaft.
  • iNDr cooling partition seal failure — Torn or missing partition seals in the iNDr cooling system cause rapid coolant temperature rise. Inspect seals during every 500-hour service.

Undercarriage — SK200-8 Specifications

The SK200-8 uses a standard 49-link track configuration per side. Undercarriage wear limits closely match those of the Komatsu PC200-8 due to similar machine weight and working configuration. SALT (Sealed-and-Lubricated Track) chain is available and recommended for quarry and mining applications.
