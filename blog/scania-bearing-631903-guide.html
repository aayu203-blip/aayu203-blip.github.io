What Is Scania Part 631903?

Scania part number 631903 is a ball bearing used in Scania truck driveline and axle assemblies. It is classified under HSN 84822011 — ball bearings for motor vehicles — and is manufactured to OEM dimensional tolerances and material specifications.

Bearings of this type operate under sustained radial and axial load, often in high-heat environments within axle housings or gearbox casings. When they wear, the consequences are progressive at first — noise, heat, slight vibration — and then potentially sudden, resulting in axle or gearbox failure if the bearing collapses completely.

Signs of Bearing Failure

Scania bearing failures rarely happen without warning. Key symptoms to watch for:

  1. Humming or rumbling noise — a persistent low-frequency noise that changes pitch with vehicle speed. This is the most common early warning. The noise typically rises and falls with road speed, not engine RPM.
  2. Excessive heat at the hub or axle housing — after a run, check bearing housings by touch. A bearing running beyond its load or lubrication limits runs significantly hotter than surrounding components.
  3. Vibration under load — noticeable in the cab, particularly when the truck is loaded and at highway speed. A failing driveline bearing creates vibration that travels through the chassis.
  4. Grease or oil leakage around the bearing housing — a seizing or overheated bearing can force grease out through seals, or cause seal lip damage that allows leakage.
  5. Play or roughness when rotating the hub by hand — with the wheel removed and the handbrake off, any roughness, catching, or axial play indicates a bearing that needs replacement.
