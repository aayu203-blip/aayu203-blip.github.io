The Situation on the Ground

Road infrastructure contracts in East Africa operate on tight timelines. A contractor working on a Kenyan government road rehabilitation project had a CAT 140M motor grader fail mid-project due to a failed circle drive assembly — the ring gear mechanism that rotates the blade.

The circle drive on a CAT 140M is a specialist component. It is not the kind of part that regional CAT dealers in East Africa typically hold in stock. When the contractor's procurement team began calling suppliers, they were getting consistent responses: 6 to 8 weeks lead time through official OEM channels. The project timeline did not have 6 to 8 weeks.

The machine was immobile. The project was costing the contractor daily penalties. Someone in their network suggested PTC.

The Phone Call to PTC

The contractor's project manager sent PTC a WhatsApp message with the machine model, the serial number, and the failed component description at approximately 9 AM Mumbai time. Within two hours, PTC had:

  • Confirmed the correct part number against the machine's serial number range
  • Verified stock availability in the Mumbai warehouse
  • Issued a proforma invoice with DHL Express pricing to Nairobi

The proforma was accepted and payment confirmed by early afternoon. PTC's team packed and dispatched the assembly via DHL Express the same day — catching the afternoon outbound flight.

The Customs Dimension

Shipping to Kenya — or anywhere in East Africa — involves careful documentation. Kenyan customs requires a commercial invoice, packing list, and certificate of origin. For machinery parts above certain values, a Pre-Export Verification of Conformity (PVoC) certificate may also be required under the Kenya Bureau of Standards regime.

PTC has 70 years of export experience across 50+ countries, including regular shipments into Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Nigeria, Ghana, and other African markets. The documentation set was prepared correctly and dispatched with the shipment. The assembly cleared Nairobi customs on day three.

The part was fitted and the grader was back in operation on day four from the initial call.

Why Circle Drives Are Hard to Source Quickly

The CAT 140M circle drive is a slow-moving part for most suppliers. The failure mode — wear and fracture of the ring gear under load — is common in graders working in rough terrain or where the blade is used for heavy cutting work. But because it doesn't fail frequently compared to filters or seals, most regional dealers don't hold it.
