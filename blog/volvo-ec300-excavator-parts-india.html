Volvo EC300 / EC360 — Large Excavators for Heavy Indian Infrastructure

The Volvo EC300 and EC360 are the large-class excavators that Volvo Construction Equipment competes against the CAT 336 and Komatsu PC360 with in the Indian market. At 30–37 tonnes, they are used for large road cutting, bridge foundation excavation, rock quarrying, and port dredging support operations. While lower in volume than the EC210 in the Indian fleet, the EC300/EC360 represent high-value parts demand — these machines carry large hydraulic systems and demanding engine specifications.

The active generations are EC300D (2014–2019), EC360E (2019–present), and the legacy EC360B/C which many Indian operators purchased before the D/E generation. This guide covers the D8 engine series and the high-pressure hydraulic system.

EC300/EC360 Engine — Volvo D8H and D8J

The EC300D uses the Volvo D8H engine — an 8.0-litre six-cylinder turbocharged unit producing 170 kW (228 hp). The D8H is a common-rail engine meeting Tier 4 Interim / Stage IIIB requirements. The EC360E uses the updated D8J engine meeting Tier 4 Final / Stage V, with revised aftertreatment including a diesel particulate filter.

Key D8H/D8J maintenance points: EGR cooler service at 3,000 hours (Indian fuel accelerates fouling), coolant analysis at every filter change (D8 coolant system is sensitive to inhibitor depletion), and turbocharger oil line inspection at 2,000 hours.

Large Hydraulic System — EC300/EC360

The EC300/EC360 uses a high-flow hydraulic system appropriate to a 30-37 tonne machine. Main pump case drain flow measurement (normal: below 10 L/min per section at rated speed) is the key diagnostic indicator. The hydraulic cooler requires more frequent cleaning than smaller machines — at 250-hour intervals in dusty quarry conditions versus 500 hours for an EC210.

Cylinder bore dimensions are larger than the EC210: boom cylinders use 150 mm bore versus 125 mm on the EC210. Confirm the correct cylinder seal kit by model and generation before ordering — EC300D and EC360E seals are not interchangeable with EC210 sizes.
