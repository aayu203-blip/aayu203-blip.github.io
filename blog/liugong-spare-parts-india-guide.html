Why LiuGong Equipment is Growing in India

LiuGong Machinery (广西柳工机械股份有限公司), founded in 1958 in Liuzhou, China, is one of the world's top 10 construction equipment manufacturers. In India, LiuGong excavators and backhoe loaders have grown rapidly since 2015 — driven by competitive pricing, solid build quality, and a widening dealer network across Maharashtra, Gujarat, Rajasthan, and the South.

The CLG series excavators (CLG906 through CLG970) are now common on road construction, mining, and real estate sites across India. The CLG766 backhoe loader competes directly with JCB 3DX and CAT 428. As the installed base grows, so does the demand for genuine replacement parts — and that's where the supply chain has historically been thin.

Most In-Demand LiuGong Spare Parts in India

Based on enquiries to Parts Trading Company, these are the parts with the highest replacement frequency on LiuGong equipment operating in Indian conditions:

Hydraulic Parts

LiuGong excavators use hydraulic systems from Kawasaki (K3SP36C, PSVD2-27E) and Linde. The pilot gear pump — which controls the pilot circuit pressure — is the most commonly replaced hydraulic component after 5,000–8,000 hours. Swivel joints (also called centre joints or rotary joints) wear out on machines that do a lot of travel on rough terrain, which is typical on Indian construction sites.

  • Pilot Gear Pump for CLG906 (PSVD2-27E)
  • Pilot Gear Pump for CLG908 (K3SP36C)
  • Centre Swivel Joint — CLG906
  • Centre Swivel Joint — CLG908
  • Centre Swivel Joint — CLG225
  • Centre Swivel Joint — CLG925 / CLG915 / CLG920
  • Boom Cylinder Seal Kit — 922D

Engine Parts — CLG766 Backhoe (Deutz TD226B-4)

The CLG766 uses a Deutz TD226B-4 four-cylinder air-cooled diesel. This engine is well-proven but requires genuine overhaul gaskets and connecting rods at major service intervals. Counterfeit gasket kits are common in the Indian market — a failed head gasket on a working backhoe costs far more in downtime than the saving on the part.

  • Engine Overhaul Gasket Kit — CLG766 / Deutz TD226B-4
  • Connecting Rod — CLG766 / Deutz TD226B-4

Cab & Body Parts

Door and engine cover locks are consumables on any machine used on active sites. The LG915/LG922/LG925 excavators use a common lock mechanism — the same key works both the door side lock and engine cover lock, which makes parts management easier.

How to Identify Your LiuGong Part Number

LiuGong part numbers follow a structured format. The most reliable way to find the correct part is:

  1. Machine nameplate — located in the cab, usually on the door sill or behind the operator seat. Note the full model (e.g., CLG922D) and serial number (e.g., 922D200001).
  2. Parts book / IPL — LiuGong provides Illustrated Parts Lists (IPL) through authorised dealers. If you have the IPL, match the assembly diagram to the part number.
  3. WhatsApp the part to PTC — send us the machine model, serial, and a photo of the worn part. Our technical team confirms the correct part number before dispatch. This is the fastest route for most Indian operators who don't have the IPL on hand.

Genuine vs Aftermarket LiuGong Parts — What to Know

LiuGong uses a mix of OEM-supplied components (Kawasaki hydraulics, Cummins engines, Bosch electrical) and LiuGong-branded parts. For major components — hydraulic pumps, swivel joints, engine internals — we recommend genuine or OEM-specification parts only. For consumables (filters, seals, belts), quality aftermarket is acceptable if sourced from reputable manufacturers.

The risk with cheap counterfeit hydraulic parts in India is real: a failed pilot pump can cause the main pump to run dry, leading to a ₹3–5 lakh main pump replacement. The genuine pilot pump costs far less than that.
