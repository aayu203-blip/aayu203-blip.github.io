What Is Part 20390836?

Volvo part number 20390836 is a front axle rubber spring — also referred to in workshop manuals as a suspension buffer. It is a rubber-and-steel block that sits between the front axle beam and the chassis rail on Volvo FH, FM, FMX, and F-series trucks.

Its job is mechanical but critical: absorb vertical road shock before it reaches the chassis, damp out vibration, and prevent metal-to-metal contact between the axle and frame when the truck hits a bump or carries a heavy load. Without this component, every kerb, pothole, and load shift transfers directly as shock into the chassis — accelerating fatigue cracking and cab wear.

Failure Symptoms

Rubber springs don't fail suddenly — they degrade over time. Watch for:

  1. Increased vibration through the cab and steering wheel — especially at highway speed. The rubber is no longer absorbing chassis flex.
  2. Clunking from the front axle area — a dull metallic impact sound over bumps, speed humps, or rough terrain. This is the axle beam contacting the chassis without adequate buffering.
  3. Uneven or accelerated front tyre wear — visible as wear on one side of the tread pattern, caused by front axle geometry shifts when the spring is no longer holding its shape.
  4. Visible cracking or deformation — inspect the rubber directly. Radial cracks, splitting at the steel insert, or permanent compression (the rubber no longer returns to its original shape) all indicate replacement is needed.
  5. Deteriorated handling under heavy load — the truck feels vague or floaty when loaded, especially during cornering.

Replacement Tips

A few practical points from our technical team:

  • Replace in pairs. If one rubber spring is worn, the other has covered the same kilometres under the same conditions. Fitting one new and one worn spring creates uneven front axle geometry.
  • Check the surrounding components. When removing the rubber spring, inspect the spring seats, leaf spring clips, and U-bolts for corrosion or cracking. Replace together if necessary.
  • Torque to spec. Refer to your Volvo workshop manual for the correct torque values on axle clamp bolts. Over-tightening can pre-compress the rubber and reduce its service life.
  • OEM vs aftermarket. OEM-specification aftermarket rubber springs match the original in compound hardness (durometer), dimensions, and insert design. Generic alternatives often use softer rubber that compresses out prematurely.
