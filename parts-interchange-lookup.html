About Interchange Lookups

What is a part number interchange or cross-reference?
An interchange or cross-reference is a mapping between an OEM (original equipment manufacturer) part number and equivalent aftermarket or alternate-brand part numbers that fit the same application. Manufacturers often reuse the same physical component across multiple machine models and even across brands under license, so one physical part can have several valid part numbers.
Is an aftermarket part with a different number the same as the OEM part?
Aftermarket parts are manufactured to match OEM specifications (dimensions, material, tolerances) but carry a different part number from a different manufacturer. A correct cross-reference means the aftermarket part fits and performs the same function as the OEM part it replaces — always confirm fitment against your machine's serial number before ordering.
