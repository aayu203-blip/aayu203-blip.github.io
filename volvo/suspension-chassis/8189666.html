Failure Symptoms

A loose or sluggish pedal that does not return to its normal position promptly is a primary indicator of a failing spring pedal. If ignored, the pedal may fail to return completely, potentially causing issues with clutch disengagement or brake application. This can lead to unintended vehicle movement or reduced braking effectiveness, posing a safety risk.

Installation Tip

Before removing the old spring, note its exact orientation and attachment points to ensure correct installation of the new one. Avoid stretching the new spring excessively during installation, as this can pre-load it and reduce its lifespan.

Service Note

Inspect the pedal pivot points and bushings for wear or binding when replacing the spring pedal.
