What It Is

The rear axle wheel hub that carries the wheel bearings, mounts the brake disc or drum, and provides the wheel stud mounting flange. The hub is the central rotating component of the rear axle assembly.

Why Parts Trading Company

Symptoms of Failure

  • Wheel bearing failure due to hub bore wear
  • Wheel wobble or runout
  • Cracked or damaged hub flange
  • Stripped wheel studs
  • Hub seal failures causing grease loss.
