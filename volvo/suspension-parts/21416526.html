What It Is

A solid or hollow moulded rubber spring element used as a secondary suspension buffer or helper spring in Volvo leaf or air suspension systems. Acts as a progressive-rate spring supplement or travel limiter, protecting the main suspension from bottoming-out shock.

Symptoms of Failure

  • Bottoming out on rough roads or speed bumps
  • Visible cracking, splitting, or compression set in the rubber
  • Loud impact noise from axle area under heavy load.
