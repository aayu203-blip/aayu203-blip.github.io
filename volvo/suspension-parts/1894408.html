What It Is

A bonded rubber-metal suspension bush used at pivot points in the leaf spring, trailing arm, or anti-roll bar mounting. Isolates road vibration from the chassis and allows controlled angular movement at the joint.

Symptoms of Failure

  • Knocking or clunking from suspension on road bumps
  • Visible cracking or deformation of the rubber section
  • Increased vibration transmitted into the cab
  • Misalignment of axle or control arm after inspection.
