Failure Symptoms

The first indication of a clogged hydraulic filter may be a slight sluggishness in the machine's functions or a faint whining noise from the hydraulic pump. As the filter becomes more restricted, hydraulic performance will noticeably degrade, and the fluid temperature may rise. Prolonged operation with a blocked filter can lead to premature failure of expensive hydraulic components like pumps and cylinders.

Installation Tip

Before removing the old filter, thoroughly clean the surrounding area to prevent any debris from falling into the hydraulic tank. Ensure the new filter's gasket is properly seated and lightly lubricated to create a good seal.

Service Note

When replacing the hydraulic oil filter, it is recommended to inspect the hydraulic fluid level and condition, and consider a fluid sample analysis.
