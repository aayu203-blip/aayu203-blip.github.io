Installation & Application Notes

Never run an engine without a filter — even briefly causes abrasive damage. Pre-fill fuel filters with clean fuel before fitting to reduce prime time. Replace O-rings on filter housings every second service. Record change dates on filter housing.

Always consult the OEM service manual for model-specific torque values and procedures. WhatsApp our technical team if you need fitment guidance.

Failure Symptoms

A dirty air filter will first manifest as a noticeable decrease in engine power and responsiveness. As it becomes more clogged, you may observe increased fuel consumption and black smoke from the exhaust. In severe cases, prolonged use of a restricted air filter can lead to increased engine wear and reduced overall performance.

Installation Tip

Before installing the new filter, ensure the air filter housing is thoroughly cleaned of any debris to prevent contamination. Make sure the new filter is correctly oriented and seated firmly to create a proper seal.

Service Note

Inspect the air intake hoses and clamps for any signs of damage or leaks when replacing the air filter.
