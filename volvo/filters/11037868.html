Failure Symptoms

Early indications of a clogged hydraulic filter can include a slight hesitation or sluggishness in hydraulic functions. As the filter becomes more restricted, hydraulic systems may exhibit jerky movements, reduced lifting capacity, or audible whining from the pump. If left unreplaced, a severely blocked filter can lead to overheating of the hydraulic fluid and accelerated wear on critical components, potentially causing system failure.

Installation Tip

Before removing the old filter, clean the area around it to prevent dirt from entering the hydraulic system. When installing the new filter, ensure the O-ring seal is properly lubricated and seated correctly to prevent leaks.

Service Note

Inspect the hydraulic fluid level and condition, and check for any signs of leaks or wear in hydraulic hoses and cylinders when replacing the hydraulic filter.
