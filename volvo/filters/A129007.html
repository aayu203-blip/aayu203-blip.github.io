A129007 — Fuel Filter Volvo Fh / Fm / Fmx, Renault / Kerax / Magnum

This fuel filter is designed to remove particulate contaminants from the diesel fuel supplied to the engine in Volvo and Renault trucks. It ensures that only clean fuel reaches the high-pressure fuel pump and injectors, protecting them from abrasive wear and ensuring efficient combustion. A failing filter can lead to reduced engine performance, increased emissions, and potential damage to the fuel injection system.

Installation & Application Notes

Never run an engine without a filter — even briefly causes abrasive damage. Pre-fill fuel filters with clean fuel before fitting to reduce prime time. Replace O-rings on filter housings every second service. Record change dates on filter housing.

Always consult the OEM service manual for model-specific torque values and procedures. WhatsApp our technical team if you need fitment guidance.

Failure Symptoms

A clogged fuel filter can initially cause a slight hesitation when accelerating or a noticeable reduction in engine power, especially when climbing hills. If the restriction continues, the engine may start to run rough, misfire, or even stall under load. Prolonged use of a clogged filter can lead to premature wear of the fuel pump and injectors.

Installation Tip

Ensure the area around the fuel filter housing is clean before removal to prevent dirt from entering the fuel lines. Always prime the fuel system after installation to remove any air pockets.

Service Note

It is advisable to replace the fuel pre-filter (if equipped) at the same time as the main fuel filter.
