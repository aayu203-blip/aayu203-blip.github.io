Installation & Application Notes

Never run an engine without a filter — even briefly causes abrasive damage. Pre-fill fuel filters with clean fuel before fitting to reduce prime time. Replace O-rings on filter housings every second service. Record change dates on filter housing.

Failure Symptoms

A dirty air filter can lead to a noticeable loss of engine power and responsiveness, particularly when accelerating or climbing grades. If ignored, restricted airflow can cause increased fuel consumption, excessive black smoke from the exhaust, and potentially lead to engine overheating or internal damage over time. This can significantly impact productivity and increase maintenance costs.

Installation Tip

Before installing the new filter, carefully clean the air filter housing to remove any accumulated debris that could compromise the seal. Ensure the filter is seated correctly and the housing is securely closed to prevent unfiltered air from entering the engine.

Service Note

Inspect the air intake piping and any associated sensors for damage or blockages while replacing the air filter.
