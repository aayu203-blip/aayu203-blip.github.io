Always replace brakes in axle sets. Bed-in new brake pads with 8–10 moderate stops from 40 km/h. Check disc runout with a dial gauge — maximum 0.1 mm. Inspect caliper slides and pistons when replacing pads.

Failure Symptoms

Initial symptoms of a failing air brake compressor include a longer time to build air pressure, audible air leaks, or the compressor running continuously without reaching full pressure. If ignored, the system may not have enough air pressure to apply the brakes effectively, especially after multiple brake applications. The most critical consequence is the complete loss of braking ability, posing an extreme risk to vehicle safety and surrounding traffic.

Installation Tip

Ensure the drive belt is properly tensioned after installation; a loose belt will cause slippage and inadequate air production. Do not overtighten the mounting bolts, as this can distort the compressor housing.

Service Note

Replace the air dryer cartridge when installing a new air brake compressor to ensure system dryness and prevent premature wear.
