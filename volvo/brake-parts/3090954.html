Failure Symptoms

The first indication of a compromised dust cover is often visible damage, such as cracks or tears, or the presence of dirt and grime around the tappet. If ignored, contaminants can enter the adjuster mechanism, leading to increased friction and potential seizure of the tappet. The risk is premature wear of the brake adjuster components and compromised brake adjustment accuracy.

Installation Tip

Ensure the tappet shaft is clean and free of rust before attempting to install the new dust cover. Carefully work the cover over the tappet, ensuring it seats fully without tearing.

Service Note

When replacing the brake adjuster tappet dust cover, clean the tappet mechanism and inspect the brake shoes and drum for wear.
