Failure Symptoms

Initial symptoms of a failing stator shaft may include a noticeable decrease in electrical output or intermittent charging system warnings. If left unaddressed, this can lead to a complete loss of electrical power, rendering the vehicle inoperable. The risk is significant disruption to operations and potential damage to sensitive electronic components.

Installation Tip

Ensure the shaft is properly aligned with the stator housing before attempting installation to prevent binding or damage to the splines. Do not use excessive force during assembly; it should slide into place smoothly.

Service Note

When replacing the stator shaft, it is recommended to inspect the condition of the bearings and seals for any signs of wear or leakage.
