Failure Symptoms

The initial indication of a faulty AdBlue level sensor is an inaccurate or fluctuating reading of the Diesel Exhaust Fluid level on the dashboard display. If this issue persists, the vehicle may display a persistent warning light or even reduce engine power to enforce emissions compliance. The ultimate consequence of ignoring a faulty sensor is the inability to operate the vehicle legally due to emissions system faults.

Installation Tip

Ensure the new sensor is properly seated and sealed to prevent any leaks of AdBlue fluid from the tank. Do not operate the vehicle until the AdBlue tank has been refilled and the system has had time to recalibrate.

Service Note

When replacing the AdBlue level sensor, inspect the AdBlue tank for any contamination and check the SCR system's dosing unit for proper operation.
