Failure Symptoms

A failing wheel speed sensor often triggers an ABS warning light on the dashboard. If ignored, the ABS and traction control systems may become inoperative, leading to compromised braking performance and reduced vehicle stability. The ultimate risk is an inability to control the vehicle effectively during emergency braking maneuvers.

Installation Tip

Ensure the mounting surface on the wheel hub is clean and free of debris before installing the new sensor. Avoid forcing the sensor into its mounting hole, which can damage the sensor or the hub assembly.

Service Note

When replacing a wheel speed sensor, inspect the brake pads, rotors, and ABS ring for wear or damage.
