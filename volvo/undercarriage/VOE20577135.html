Failure Symptoms

Initial signs of a faulty ECU controller panel may include intermittent display issues, warning lights, or unresponsive controls. If left unaddressed, critical machine functions could become unreliable or cease to operate, leading to operational downtime. Continued use with a malfunctioning panel can potentially cause damage to other integrated control modules.

Installation Tip

Always disconnect the battery before removing or installing the ECU controller panel to prevent electrical damage. Ensure all connectors are securely seated and locked into place to maintain proper electrical contact.

Service Note

Inspect the wiring harness and all related sensors and actuators when replacing the ECU controller panel.
