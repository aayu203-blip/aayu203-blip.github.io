Failure Symptoms

Early signs of failing track rollers include increased noise and vibration transmitted through the paver chassis. If ignored, the rollers can seize or wear unevenly, leading to accelerated damage to the track chains and sprockets. The ultimate consequence is potential track derailment, causing significant downtime and compromising the quality of the paved surface.

Installation Tip

Ensure the track is properly de-tensioned before attempting to remove or install track rollers to avoid injury. When mounting new rollers, use the correct fasteners and torque specifications to prevent them from loosening or causing damage to the mounting points.

Service Note

Inspect the track chains, sprockets, and tensioning system for wear or damage whenever replacing track rollers.
