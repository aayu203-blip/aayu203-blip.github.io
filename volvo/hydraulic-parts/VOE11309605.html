Failure Symptoms

Initial symptoms of a failing hydraulic pump include sluggish or weak hydraulic functions and inconsistent system pressure. If left unaddressed, the pump's performance will degrade further, potentially leading to a complete loss of hydraulic power and inability to operate the machine. This can result in significant downtime and costly repairs.

Installation Tip

Thoroughly flush the hydraulic system and inspect all lines and components for contamination before installing the new pump. Ensure the pump shaft is properly aligned and lubricated before mating it to the drive source.

Service Note

Replace the hydraulic filter element and inspect the hydraulic fluid for signs of wear or contamination when replacing the pump.
