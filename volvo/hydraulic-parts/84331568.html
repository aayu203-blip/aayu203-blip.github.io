Failure Symptoms

The initial symptom of a failing hose is often a visible external leak of hydraulic fluid or a noticeable hissing sound if it's an air hose. If left unaddressed, the leak can worsen, leading to a significant loss of system pressure and potential component damage. A burst hose can cause immediate and complete loss of function in the affected system, posing a safety hazard.

Installation Tip

Ensure all hose fittings are clean and free of debris before connecting the new hose to prevent contamination of the system. Avoid twisting the hose during installation, as this can restrict flow and cause premature failure.

Service Note

Inspect all adjacent hoses and hydraulic lines for signs of wear, abrasion, or leaks when replacing a hose.
