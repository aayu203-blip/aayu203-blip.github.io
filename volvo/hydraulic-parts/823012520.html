Failure Symptoms

Initial symptoms may include a persistent whining sound from the hydraulic pump or a noticeable drop in hydraulic system performance. If left unaddressed, the valve can fail to regulate pressure, potentially leading to over-pressurization and damage to hydraulic pumps, cylinders, and hoses.

Installation Tip

Ensure the hydraulic system is properly depressurized before attempting to remove the old valve. Be careful not to cross-thread the valve when installing the new one.

Service Note

Inspect the hydraulic fluid for debris and check the hydraulic pump for signs of wear when replacing the relief valve.
