Failure Symptoms

The initial symptom of failing boom lift cylinder seals is often a slow leak of hydraulic fluid from around the cylinder rod or a gradual loss of lifting height when the engine is off. If ignored, the leaks will increase, leading to reduced lifting capacity, slower boom operation, and potential overheating of the hydraulic system. Continued operation with leaking seals can cause damage to the cylinder bore and piston, leading to more costly repairs.

Installation Tip

Clean the cylinder bore and piston rod meticulously before installing the new seals to prevent contamination and ensure a tight seal. Be careful not to nick or damage the new seals during installation.

Service Note

Inspect the boom cylinder rod for any signs of pitting or damage, and check the hydraulic fluid for contamination before replacing the seal kit.
