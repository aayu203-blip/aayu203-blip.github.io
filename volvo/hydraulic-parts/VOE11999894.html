VOE11999894 — Boom Lift Cylinder Seal Kit Voe11999894 For Volvo Loader L120C L120D

This is a boom lift cylinder seal kit designed to ensure the proper functioning of the hydraulic system in Volvo wheel loaders. It prevents hydraulic fluid from escaping the boom lift cylinder, maintaining consistent lifting force and preventing contamination. A failed seal kit will manifest as external fluid leaks around the cylinder rod and a noticeable reduction in the loader's lifting capacity.

Failure Symptoms

The most common symptom of a failing boom lift cylinder seal kit is visible hydraulic fluid leaking from the cylinder rod. This will lead to a gradual loss of lifting power, making it difficult for the loader to raise its bucket or attachments. If left unaddressed, the cylinder may lose pressure entirely, preventing the boom from being raised or held in position.

Installation Tip

Carefully inspect the cylinder rod for any imperfections or scoring before installing the new seals, as these can cause premature failure. Ensure all old seal material is completely removed from the cylinder grooves.

Service Note

Inspect the boom lift cylinder rod for any signs of wear or damage that could affect the performance of the new seals.
