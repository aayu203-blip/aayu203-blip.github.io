Failure Symptoms

Early signs of failure include intermittent or delayed response from hydraulic functions, such as the boom not immediately lifting or the swing being jerky. If ignored, the affected hydraulic function may cease to operate entirely, or the valve may stick in one position, causing unintended machine movement. This can lead to operational downtime and potential damage if the machine behaves unexpectedly.

Installation Tip

Ensure the machine's electrical system is de-energized before disconnecting or connecting the solenoid valve to prevent electrical damage. Do not force the valve into its mounting port; ensure it is properly seated.

Service Note

Inspect the hydraulic fluid level and check for any debris in the hydraulic filter when replacing a solenoid valve.
