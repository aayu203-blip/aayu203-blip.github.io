What It Is

A hydraulic swivel joint or banjo fitting that allows relative rotation or articulation between two hydraulic lines or between a rigid line and a component. Prevents line stress fracture at points where movement occurs (e.g., at the brake caliper, steering cylinder, or suspension-mounted hydraulic component).

Symptoms of Failure

  • Hydraulic fluid leak at swivel joint
  • Stress crack in rigid tube at the swivel connection
  • Restricted swivel rotation causing tube fatigue.
