Failure Symptoms

A failing solenoid valve may initially cause intermittent issues, such as a hydraulic function that works only sometimes or responds slowly. If left unaddressed, the valve can become completely stuck, preventing the operation of critical systems and potentially leading to a loss of control over machine movements. This can create a hazardous working environment and impede productivity.

Installation Tip

Ensure the electrical connector is clean and properly seated before installing the new valve to guarantee a good electrical connection. Avoid forcing the valve into its mounting position, as this could damage the valve body or the machine's hydraulic manifold.

Service Note

Inspect the hydraulic fluid and filter for any signs of contamination, as this can affect the operation of solenoid valves.
