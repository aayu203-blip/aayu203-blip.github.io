Failure Symptoms

The first indication of a faulty valve is inconsistent or delayed response in the controlled system. If ignored, the valve may fail to reset, leading to the system remaining in an unintended state. This can result in compromised braking performance or loss of control over critical functions.

Installation Tip

Ensure all air lines are properly connected and secured to prevent leaks, which can affect the valve's auto-reset function. Avoid overtightening fittings, as this can damage the valve body.

Service Note

Inspect associated air lines and solenoids for damage or wear when replacing this valve.
