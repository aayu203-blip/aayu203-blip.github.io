Failure Symptoms

Early signs of an oil pump issue may include a flickering oil pressure warning light or slightly lower than normal oil pressure readings. If the problem progresses, the oil pressure can drop significantly, leading to increased engine noise and potential overheating. Failure to address a failing oil pump can result in severe engine damage due to lack of lubrication.

Installation Tip

Ensure the oil pickup tube screen is clean and properly seated in the oil pan before installing the new pump. Do not reuse old gaskets or seals, as they can lead to leaks.

Service Note

Inspect the engine oil pan and pickup screen for debris, and replace the engine oil and filter when servicing the oil pump.
