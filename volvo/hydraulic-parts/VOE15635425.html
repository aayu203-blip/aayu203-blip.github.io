Failure Symptoms

Initial symptoms of a failing joystick valve include a slight stiffness in joystick movement or minor delays in hydraulic response. If ignored, the valve may cause jerky movements, difficulty maintaining precise positions, or even unintended operation of excavator functions, impacting productivity and safety.

Installation Tip

Ensure all hydraulic lines are properly labeled before disconnecting them to avoid incorrect reassembly. Never force connections; if a fitting is difficult to attach, re-check the line and port alignment.

Service Note

Inspect the hydraulic pilot lines and the main hydraulic pump for any signs of wear or leakage when replacing the joystick control valve.
