Failure Symptoms

The first indication of a failing hydraulic pump is often a noticeable decrease in the speed and power of hydraulic functions. This may be accompanied by a whining or groaning sound from the pump. If left unaddressed, the machine may become inoperable due to a complete loss of hydraulic pressure, leading to significant project delays.

Installation Tip

Thoroughly flush the hydraulic system and replace the hydraulic filter before installing the new pump to remove any debris that could cause premature failure. Ensure the pump shaft is properly aligned with the drive coupling.

Service Note

When replacing the hydraulic pump, it is crucial to inspect the hydraulic fluid for metal particles and replace the main hydraulic filter.
