Failure Symptoms

The first sign of a failing door lock is often stiffness or difficulty in operating the handle or key cylinder. If ignored, the lock may fail to engage or disengage entirely, leaving the door unsecured or impossible to open. The risk is compromised vehicle security and potential inability to access the cabin.

Installation Tip

Ensure all linkage rods and clips are properly connected and adjusted before fully securing the interior door panel. Avoid forcing the lock mechanism if it feels binding, as this may indicate misalignment.

Service Note

Inspect the door latch mechanism, weather stripping, and interior door panel for any damage or wear that should be addressed concurrently.
