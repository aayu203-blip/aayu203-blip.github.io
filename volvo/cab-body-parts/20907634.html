Failure Symptoms

Initial signs of failure may include unusual noises like creaking or rattling from the cabin area. If left unaddressed, the issue can lead to misalignment of panels or compromised interior comfort. Continued neglect risks increased wear on adjacent components and potential water ingress.

Installation Tip

Ensure the mounting area is clean and free of debris before attempting installation. Avoid overtightening fasteners, which can strip threads or damage the mounting points.

Service Note

Inspect adjacent trim pieces, seals, and mounting hardware for wear or damage during replacement.
