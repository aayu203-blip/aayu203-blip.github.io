Failure Symptoms

A gas spring that fails to hold its intended position, or moves sluggishly, is the primary symptom. If ignored, the spring will lose its lifting force entirely, potentially causing the supported panel to slam shut unexpectedly. This poses a significant risk of injury to personnel and damage to the panel itself.

Installation Tip

Ensure the panel is securely propped open before attempting to remove the old gas spring, as it is under pressure and will no longer provide support. Align the mounting points carefully to avoid stressing the new spring during installation.

Service Note

Inspect the mounting brackets and surrounding panel structure for any signs of stress or damage when replacing the gas spring.
