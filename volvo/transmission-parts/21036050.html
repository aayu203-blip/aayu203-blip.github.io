Installation & Application Notes

Confirm whether the hub is grease-packed or oil-bath before fitting, and pack or fill accordingly. Always renew the hub oil or grease seal whenever the hub is removed for bearing replacement — reusing a disturbed seal is the most common cause of repeat failures. Set bearing preload to Volvo's specified torque and re-check wheel nut torque after the first 50–100 km.

Always consult the OEM service manual for model-specific torque values and procedures. WhatsApp our technical team if you need fitment guidance.
