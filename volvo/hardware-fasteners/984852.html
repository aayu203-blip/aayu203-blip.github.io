What It Is

An OEM-specification Grade 10.9 flanged hex screw used to bolt the propeller shaft yoke to the gearbox or axle flange on Volvo trucks. Propeller shaft flange bolts are subject to high cyclic torsional and bending loads — only OEM-grade fasteners should be used.

Why Parts Trading Company

Propeller shaft flange bolts should ALWAYS be replaced on removal — never reused. Single-use stretch-bolt or pre-torqued fasteners. OEM specification critical for safety. Pack of 4 or 6 available.
