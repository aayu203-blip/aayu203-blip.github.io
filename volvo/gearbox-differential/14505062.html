Failure Symptoms

Initial signs of a failing sun gear shaft may include slight hesitation when shifting gears or a subtle increase in transmission noise. If left unaddressed, the shaft can wear further, leading to pronounced grinding and significant power loss. Continued operation with a compromised shaft risks damage to other transmission components, necessitating more extensive and costly repairs.

Installation Tip

Ensure the transmission housing is thoroughly cleaned before installation to prevent contamination. Avoid forcing the shaft into place, as this can damage the splines or the housing bore.

Service Note

Inspect the planetary gear set and associated bearings for wear or damage when replacing the sun gear shaft.
