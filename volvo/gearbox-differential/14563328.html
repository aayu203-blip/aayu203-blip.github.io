Failure Symptoms

Initial symptoms of a failing swing gear include hesitant or jerky rotation of the excavator's upper structure. If left unaddressed, the gear teeth can wear down further, leading to increased noise and potential loss of swing function. This can significantly impede the machine's operational capability.

Installation Tip

Thoroughly clean the mating surfaces of the swing drive housing and the gearcase before installation to prevent contamination. Ensure proper lubrication of the gear teeth and splines as per the service manual before assembly.

Service Note

Inspect the swing drive motor, planetary gears, and the main swing bearing for wear or damage.
