Failure Symptoms

A failing tensioner may first manifest as a persistent squealing or flapping noise from the accessory belt area. If left unaddressed, the belt may slip intermittently, causing issues with charging or cooling, or it could lead to premature belt wear and eventual breakage. Continued operation risks damage to the belt and pulleys, potentially disabling critical engine functions.

Installation Tip

Before installing, ensure the mounting bracket and bolt threads are clean and undamaged to prevent cross-threading or improper seating. Do not use excessive force when retracting the tensioner arm to install the belt, as this can damage the internal spring mechanism.

Service Note

Always inspect the condition of the auxiliary drive belt and all associated pulleys for wear or damage when replacing the tensioner assembly.
