Failure Symptoms

The first indication of a failing piston kit is often a gradual loss of engine power and a noticeable increase in oil consumption. This may progress to rough idling, misfires, and the emission of blue smoke from the exhaust due to oil entering the combustion chamber. Continued operation can lead to severe cylinder wall damage and potential piston seizure.

Installation Tip

Carefully inspect the cylinder walls for any scoring or damage before installing the new piston and rings; hone or bore if necessary. Ensure the piston rings are correctly gapped and oriented according to the service manual to prevent blow-by and oil consumption.

Service Note

Inspect the connecting rod bearings and crankshaft journals for wear when replacing piston kits, as these components are accessed during the same service procedure.
