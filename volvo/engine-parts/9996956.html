Failure Symptoms

This is a tool, not a component that fails in operation. However, a worn or damaged tool could lead to slippage, potentially causing damage to the crankshaft pulley or the tool itself. Using a damaged tool risks incomplete or inaccurate engine adjustments, which could lead to poor running conditions or component damage.

Installation Tip

Ensure the tool is fully seated onto the crankshaft pulley bolt head before applying force to prevent slippage. Do not use excessive force; rotate the engine smoothly and deliberately.

Service Note

When using this tool for timing, always verify the camshaft timing marks are also correctly aligned.
