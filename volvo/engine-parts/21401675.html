Failure Symptoms

The battery warning light illuminating on the dashboard is the most common initial symptom of an alternator problem. This can progress to intermittent electrical failures, such as flickering lights or non-responsive gauges, and eventually lead to a completely discharged battery. If left unaddressed, the vehicle will eventually lose all electrical power and stop running.

Installation Tip

Ensure the drive belt is properly tensioned after installation to prevent slippage and premature wear. Double-check all electrical connections to ensure they are secure and free from corrosion.

Service Note

When replacing the alternator, inspect the battery terminals and cables for corrosion and test the battery's state of charge.
