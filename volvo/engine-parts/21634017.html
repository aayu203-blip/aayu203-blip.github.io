Failure Symptoms

An illuminated check engine light is often the first sign of a faulty crankcase pressure sensor. If ignored, the engine may begin to run rough, experience hesitation during acceleration, or show a decrease in fuel efficiency. Prolonged operation with a faulty sensor can lead to increased oil consumption and potential damage to emission control components.

Installation Tip

Clean the sensor mounting port thoroughly to ensure a proper seal and prevent air leaks. Avoid overtightening the sensor during installation, as this can damage the threads or the sensor housing.

Service Note

Inspect the crankcase ventilation system, including hoses and filters, for any blockages or damage when replacing the pressure sensor.
