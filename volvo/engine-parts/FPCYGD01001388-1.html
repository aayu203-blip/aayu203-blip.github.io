Failure Symptoms

A failing fuel control valve may initially cause the engine to run rough, with noticeable hesitation during acceleration and increased exhaust emissions. If the issue progresses, the engine may begin to misfire, leading to a significant loss of power and difficulty maintaining idle speed. Continued operation with a faulty valve can result in fuel system pressure drops, potentially leading to engine stalling or failure to start.

Installation Tip

Ensure the fuel system is properly bled of air after installation to prevent air pockets that can disrupt fuel flow. Do not force the valve into position; it should seat smoothly if the correct part is being used.

Service Note

Always replace the fuel filter and inspect the fuel lines for any debris or damage when servicing the fuel control valve.
