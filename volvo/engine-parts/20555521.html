Failure Symptoms

The first indication of a failing fuel injector is often a rough or uneven engine idle and a noticeable drop in fuel economy. As the problem progresses, the engine may experience misfires, reduced power output, and increased exhaust emissions. Continued use with faulty injectors can lead to cylinder washing, increased wear on engine components, and potential damage to the catalytic converter.

Installation Tip

Ensure the injector bore in the cylinder head is perfectly clean and free of any debris or carbon buildup before installing the new injector. Avoid forcing the injector into place, as this can damage the sealing surfaces or the injector body.

Service Note

Inspect the fuel filter and fuel lines for any signs of contamination or blockage, and check the engine's compression for overall cylinder health.
