What It Is

An automatic spring-loaded belt tensioner that maintains correct belt tension on the engine accessory drive. Keeps the serpentine belt in firm contact with the alternator, water pump, air compressor, and power steering pump pulleys throughout the belt's service life.

Symptoms of Failure

  • Belt squealing on startup or acceleration
  • Belt slipping off pulley
  • Accessory systems (alternator, A/C, water pump) underperforming
  • Visible pulley wobble or seized bearing
  • Cracked or worn belt from incorrect tension.
