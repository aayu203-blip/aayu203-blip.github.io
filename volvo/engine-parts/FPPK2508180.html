Failure Symptoms

Visible fuel or oil leaks around the hose connection points are the first sign of failure. If ignored, the leaks can worsen, leading to a significant loss of fluid and potential engine overheating or lubrication issues. Continued operation with a compromised hose risks engine damage due to insufficient fluid levels.

Installation Tip

Ensure the hose routing is identical to the original to prevent stress or abrasion. Do not overtighten hose clamps, as this can damage the hose or fitting.

Service Note

Inspect all fuel and oil lines, as well as associated clamps and fittings, for signs of wear or leakage during hose replacement.
