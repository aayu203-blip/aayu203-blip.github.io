Failure Symptoms

The first sign of a failing sea water pump is often a decrease in water flow or an increase in engine operating temperature. If ignored, the engine can quickly overheat, leading to reduced performance and potential damage to internal components. Continued operation with a compromised cooling system risks severe engine damage.

Installation Tip

Ensure the new pump's impeller is lubricated with a marine-grade grease before installation to prevent dry-running damage. Avoid overtightening the mounting bolts, as this can distort the pump housing and cause leaks.

Service Note

Inspect the engine's heat exchanger and all raw water hoses for signs of corrosion or blockage when replacing the sea water pump.
