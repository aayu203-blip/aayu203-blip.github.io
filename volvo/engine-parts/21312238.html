21312238 Truck, Renault Truck Intercooler Hose — Volvo FH12

This is an intercooler hose designed to connect the turbocharger's compressed air outlet to the engine's intake manifold, facilitating charge air cooling for improved combustion efficiency. It is constructed from high-temperature, oil-resistant rubber and reinforced fabric to withstand pressure fluctuations and heat, unlike a cracked or collapsed hose that can lead to a loss of boost pressure and engine power. Operators may notice a significant loss of engine power, black smoke from the exhaust, or a hissing sound under acceleration when this hose fails.

Failure Symptoms

A failing intercooler hose typically first presents as a noticeable loss of engine power, especially under load. If left unaddressed, the leak can worsen, leading to increased exhaust smoke and potential turbocharger damage due to backpressure. Continued operation with a compromised hose significantly reduces fuel efficiency and engine performance.

Installation Tip

Ensure the connection surfaces on the turbocharger, intercooler, and pipework are clean and free of oil or debris before fitting the new hose. Do not reuse old hose clamps; always use new, properly sized clamps.

Service Note

Inspect the turbocharger, intercooler core, and all associated piping for leaks or damage when replacing the intercooler hose.
