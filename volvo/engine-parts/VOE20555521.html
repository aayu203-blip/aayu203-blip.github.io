Failure Symptoms

A failing fuel injector may first manifest as a slight hesitation during acceleration or a subtle increase in exhaust emissions. If ignored, the engine will likely develop a noticeable misfire, reduced overall power, and increased fuel consumption. Continued operation with a faulty injector can lead to incomplete combustion, potential cylinder damage, and increased stress on the emissions system.

Installation Tip

Ensure the injector seat in the cylinder head is meticulously clean before installing the new injector to prevent leaks and ensure proper sealing. Never reuse old copper sealing washers; always use new ones.

Service Note

When replacing a fuel injector, it is recommended to inspect the fuel rail pressure and check the fuel filter for any signs of clogging or contamination.
