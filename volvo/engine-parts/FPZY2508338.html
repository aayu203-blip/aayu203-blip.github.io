Failure Symptoms

Initial signs of a failing hub jaw coupling may include increased vibration felt through the operator's station or audible rattling noises during engine operation. If left unaddressed, the coupling can degrade further, leading to intermittent power loss or uneven operation of connected systems. Continued operation with a compromised coupling risks damage to the crankshaft or connected drivetrain components.

Installation Tip

Ensure the mating surfaces on both the crankshaft and the driven component are clean and free of debris before attempting installation. Avoid forcing the coupling onto the shaft; it should slide into place with appropriate alignment.

Service Note

Inspect the engine crankshaft seal and the corresponding shaft for any signs of leakage or wear during coupling replacement.
