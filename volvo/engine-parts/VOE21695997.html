Failure Symptoms

The first indication of an oil cooler issue might be an elevated oil temperature reading on the dashboard gauge. If the problem persists, the engine may start to run hotter overall, and oil pressure could begin to drop. Ignoring a faulty oil cooler risks severe engine damage due to overheating and inadequate lubrication.

Installation Tip

Thoroughly clean the engine oil passages and the mounting surface for the cooler to ensure a proper seal and prevent leaks. Ensure the new seals are correctly positioned before tightening the cooler assembly.

Service Note

Inspect the engine coolant level and condition, as well as the radiator and fan, for any issues when replacing the oil cooler.
