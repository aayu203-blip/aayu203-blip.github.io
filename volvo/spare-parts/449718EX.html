Failure Symptoms

The initial symptom of a failing door lock is difficulty in latching or unlatching the door, or intermittent operation. If ignored, the lock may cease to function entirely, leaving the door unable to be secured. This poses a security risk and can lead to the door opening unexpectedly while driving.

Installation Tip

Ensure all electrical connections are clean and secure before reassembling the door panel, as poor connections can cause intermittent lock operation. Do not force the mechanism during installation; if it binds, check for obstructions or misalignment.

Service Note

Inspect the door seals and weatherstripping for damage, as worn seals can contribute to door alignment issues that affect lock function.
