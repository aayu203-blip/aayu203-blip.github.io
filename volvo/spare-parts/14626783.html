Failure Symptoms

The most common symptom of a failing hour meter is a complete lack of display or an inability to advance the recorded hours. In some cases, the display may freeze on an incorrect number or show erratic readings. If the hour meter fails, it becomes impossible to accurately track service intervals, potentially leading to missed maintenance and increased wear on critical components.

Installation Tip

Ensure the electrical connections are clean and secure to prevent intermittent readings or failure to operate. Double-check the wiring diagram for correct polarity before connecting the new gauge.

Service Note

Inspect the wiring harness and connectors leading to the hour meter for any signs of damage or corrosion.
