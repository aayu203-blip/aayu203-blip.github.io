Failure Symptoms

The initial symptom of a failing oil cooler is often a gradual increase in hydraulic fluid or engine oil temperature. If left unaddressed, this can lead to reduced system performance, sluggish operation, and potential damage to pumps and seals. Prolonged operation at elevated temperatures can cause premature wear on critical components.

Installation Tip

Ensure all mating surfaces for hoses and fittings are perfectly clean and free of old sealant or debris. Always use new O-rings or seals when connecting hoses to prevent leaks.

Service Note

When replacing the oil cooler, it is essential to flush the entire hydraulic or lubrication system to remove any contaminants or debris.
