Failure Symptoms

A failing air compressor may initially present as a longer-than-usual time to build air pressure in the system. If ignored, insufficient air supply can lead to brake system warnings and reduced braking performance. A complete compressor failure can result in the inability to build sufficient air pressure for safe operation of the vehicle.

Installation Tip

Thoroughly clean the air intake line and ensure it is properly sealed to prevent air leaks before connecting the new compressor. Avoid overtightening the mounting bolts, which can damage the compressor housing or mounting surface.

Service Note

Inspect the air dryer, air governor, and all air lines for leaks or damage when replacing the air compressor.
