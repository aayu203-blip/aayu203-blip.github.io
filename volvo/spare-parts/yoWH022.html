Failure Symptoms

The initial indication of a failing hydraulic oil cooler assembly is often a visible leak of hydraulic fluid from the unit or its connections. If not addressed, this can lead to a significant drop in hydraulic fluid level and increased operating temperatures, potentially causing sluggish hydraulic functions. Prolonged operation with an inefficient cooler risks severe damage to hydraulic pumps and actuators.

Installation Tip

Ensure all mating surfaces for the cooler assembly are perfectly clean and flat to guarantee a leak-free seal. Avoid applying excessive force when connecting hydraulic lines, as this can damage the fittings or the cooler itself.

Service Note

Replace the hydraulic system filter element concurrently with the cooler assembly to ensure system cleanliness.
