Failure Symptoms

Visible hydraulic fluid leaks around the component where the seal is located are the most common initial symptom. This can lead to a gradual or sudden loss of hydraulic system pressure, resulting in slower or unresponsive operation of the loader's functions. Prolonged operation with a leaking seal can cause fluid starvation and damage to pumps or other hydraulic components.

Installation Tip

Lubricate the sealing ring with the appropriate system fluid before installation to prevent tearing or damage during insertion. Ensure the ring is seated evenly and fully within its groove.

Service Note

When replacing a sealing ring, always inspect the mating surface for any damage or wear that could compromise the new seal's effectiveness.
