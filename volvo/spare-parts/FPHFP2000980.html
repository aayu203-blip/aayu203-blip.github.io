Failure Symptoms

The initial symptom is often a visible hydraulic fluid leak at the connection point during operation. If left unaddressed, the leak may worsen, leading to difficulty in connecting or disconnecting the lines, and a potential loss of hydraulic system pressure. The ultimate risk is an inability to operate hydraulic attachments due to insufficient pressure or fluid loss.

Installation Tip

Ensure the coupler faces are clean and free of debris before attempting to connect them to prevent seal damage. Never attempt to force a connection; if it's difficult, inspect for obstructions or misalignment.

Service Note

Inspect the hydraulic hoses connected to the couplers for any signs of wear, cracking, or bulging, and check the hydraulic fluid level.
