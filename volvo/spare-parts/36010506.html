Failure Symptoms

Audible clunking or grinding noises when shifting between drive modes or turning are common initial signs. If left unaddressed, the transfer case may fail to engage 4WD, leading to reduced traction in slippery conditions. Continued operation with a compromised transfer case can result in significant drivetrain damage and costly repairs.

Installation Tip

Ensure the mating surfaces on the transmission and transfer case are perfectly clean and free of old gasket material before assembly. Do not reuse old mounting hardware; use new bolts and ensure proper alignment to prevent binding.

Service Note

Inspect and refill the transfer case with the correct type and amount of fluid, and check the condition of the driveshaft U-joints.
