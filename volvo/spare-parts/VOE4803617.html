Failure Symptoms

A failing impulse relay may initially cause intermittent operation of the electrical component it controls, such as lights or a motor. If the relay contacts become pitted or fail to make connection, the controlled circuit will cease to function entirely. In some cases, a shorted relay can overload the control circuit, potentially causing damage to the control module or wiring.

Installation Tip

Before removing the old relay, ensure the ignition is off and disconnect the battery to prevent electrical shorts. Note the terminal configuration to ensure the new relay is plugged in correctly.

Service Note

When replacing a relay, it is good practice to inspect the associated wiring and connectors for any signs of corrosion or damage.
