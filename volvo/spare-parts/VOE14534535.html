Failure Symptoms

The initial symptom of a failing swivel joint is often noticeable stiffness or jerky movements during boom and arm operation. This can progress to hydraulic fluid leaks at the joint's connection points and a gradual loss of hydraulic power to the attachment. Continued use risks significant hydraulic system contamination and potential loss of control.

Installation Tip

Thoroughly clean the mating surfaces of the swivel joint and the machine frame before installation to prevent contamination. Ensure all hydraulic lines are properly connected and bled according to the service manual.

Service Note

Inspect the hydraulic pump, cylinders, and hoses for any signs of wear or damage when replacing the center swivel joint.
