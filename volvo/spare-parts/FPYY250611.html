Failure Symptoms

An initial sign of failure may be an illuminated hydraulic system warning light or inconsistent gauge readings. If ignored, the system may begin to operate erratically or shut down due to perceived pressure faults. The equipment could become inoperable, preventing necessary work from being completed.

Installation Tip

Ensure the port threads are clean and free from debris before attempting to thread the new sensor in. Overtightening can damage the sensor housing or the mating port.

Service Note

Inspect hydraulic fluid levels and condition, and check associated wiring and connectors for damage when replacing the pressure monitor.
