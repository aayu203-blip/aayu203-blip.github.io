Failure Symptoms

Initial signs of a failing A/C compressor may include a noticeable decrease in cooling efficiency or intermittent operation of the air conditioning. If ignored, the compressor may begin to make loud grinding or knocking noises, and the system will eventually cease to produce cold air. Continued operation can lead to refrigerant contamination and potential damage to other A/C system components.

Installation Tip

Always ensure the A/C system is properly evacuated before removing the old compressor to prevent refrigerant release. Do not overtighten the compressor mounting bolts, as this can damage the compressor housing.

Service Note

It is highly recommended to replace the receiver drier and expansion valve, and to flush the A/C system when installing a new compressor.
