Failure Symptoms

Visible wear patterns or damage to the auger flighting are the first signs of impending failure. If ignored, this can lead to inconsistent asphalt mat thickness and poor surface finish. Continued operation with a worn auger risks producing substandard pavement, requiring costly rework.

Installation Tip

Ensure the auger shaft is properly aligned with the drive mechanism and that all mounting hardware is securely tightened. Do not operate the paver if the auger is not rotating freely and evenly.

Service Note

Inspect the auger bearings and seals for wear and proper lubrication during auger replacement.
