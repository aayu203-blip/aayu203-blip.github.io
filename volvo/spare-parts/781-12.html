Failure Symptoms

The most immediate symptom of a damaged or missing under bumper spoiler is increased wind noise at higher speeds and a noticeable reduction in aerodynamic efficiency. If left unaddressed, it can lead to increased fuel consumption and potentially allow road debris to impact and damage other underbody components. This poses a risk to the vehicle's long-term integrity.

Installation Tip

Thoroughly clean the bumper mounting area and ensure all existing clips or fasteners are removed before attempting to install the new spoiler. Avoid forcing the spoiler into place, as this can crack the plastic or damage the bumper.

Service Note

Inspect the front bumper and surrounding underbody panels for any signs of damage or wear when replacing the under bumper spoiler.
