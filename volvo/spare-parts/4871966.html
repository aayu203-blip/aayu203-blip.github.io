Failure Symptoms

Initial signs of worn mating plates may include a delay in gear engagement or a slight shudder when shifting. If ignored, the transmission may start to slip under load, leading to a noticeable loss of power and increased operating temperatures. Continued use with severely worn plates can result in complete loss of drive and potential damage to other transmission components.

Installation Tip

Ensure all friction and steel plates are soaked in the correct transmission fluid for at least 30 minutes before assembly to prevent initial dry engagement. Stagger the installation of new plates correctly according to the service manual to ensure even wear.

Service Note

When replacing mating plates, always inspect the transmission pump, torque converter, and clutch pack seals for wear or leaks.
