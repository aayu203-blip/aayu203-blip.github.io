Failure Symptoms

The first indication of a failing fuel injector is often a rough engine idle or a noticeable loss of power during acceleration. If left unaddressed, the engine may begin to misfire, leading to increased fuel consumption and excessive exhaust smoke. The most severe consequence is potential engine damage due to uneven combustion or fuel dilution of the engine oil.

Installation Tip

Ensure the injector seat in the cylinder head is perfectly clean and free of carbon deposits before installing the new injector; debris can cause leaks. Tighten the injector hold-down bolts evenly in stages to the manufacturer's specified torque.

Service Note

When replacing fuel injectors, it is highly recommended to replace the fuel filter and inspect the fuel lines for any signs of leaks or damage.
