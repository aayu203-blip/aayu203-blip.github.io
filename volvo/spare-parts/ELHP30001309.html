Failure Symptoms

Initial signs of a failing control lever assembly include a noticeable looseness or play in the lever, and imprecise hydraulic control. If ignored, hydraulic functions may become sluggish, difficult to modulate, or exhibit drift, impacting productivity and potentially causing minor damage to the work environment. The risk is reduced operational efficiency and potential for unintended equipment movement.

Installation Tip

Before removing the old assembly, carefully note the routing and connection of all hydraulic lines. Ensure all new connections are properly tightened to prevent hydraulic fluid leaks.

Service Note

Check the hydraulic fluid level and filter condition, and inspect hydraulic lines for leaks when replacing the control lever assembly.
