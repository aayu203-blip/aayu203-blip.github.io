Failure Symptoms

A stiff or spongy clutch pedal, difficulty engaging or disengaging gears, and potential hydraulic fluid leaks are the first signs of a failing clutch servo. If left unaddressed, the clutch may become impossible to operate, preventing the vehicle from moving. This can lead to significant downtime and potential damage to the transmission.

Installation Tip

Thoroughly flush and bleed the clutch hydraulic system after installation to remove any air and ensure proper operation. Avoid reusing old hydraulic fluid, as it can contain contaminants.

Service Note

It is recommended to inspect the clutch disc and pressure plate for wear and the clutch release bearing for smooth operation when replacing the clutch servo.
