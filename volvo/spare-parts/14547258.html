Failure Symptoms

The first symptom of a failing swing motor shaft is often increased noise or vibration during slewing operations, along with noticeable looseness in the upper structure. If ignored, the shaft can fracture, leading to a sudden loss of rotation capability and potential damage to the swing gear and motor. Continued operation with a compromised shaft risks severe damage to the entire swing drive mechanism, rendering the excavator inoperable.

Installation Tip

Ensure all bearing surfaces and gear teeth are thoroughly cleaned and lubricated before installing the new shaft to ensure proper seating and operation. Avoid using excessive force when aligning and inserting the shaft, as this could damage the gear teeth.

Service Note

Inspect the swing bearing, swing motor gears, and swing motor seals for wear or damage when replacing the swing motor shaft.
