Failure Symptoms

Initial symptoms of a failing planet carrier often include a noticeable grinding or clicking sound during turns or acceleration. If left unaddressed, the damage can progress to include significant play in the drive axle and a reduction in driving power. Ultimately, a severely damaged planet carrier can lead to a complete loss of drive to one or more wheels, immobilizing the equipment.

Installation Tip

Thoroughly clean the final drive housing and inspect all mating surfaces for damage before installing the new planet carrier. Ensure all bearing surfaces are properly lubricated according to service manual specifications.

Service Note

When replacing the planet carrier, it is highly recommended to inspect and replace the final drive seals and bearings to prevent premature failure.
