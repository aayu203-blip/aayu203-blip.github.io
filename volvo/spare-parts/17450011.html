Failure Symptoms

The first sign of a failing headlight is typically reduced light output, a cracked or fogged lens, or complete failure of the bulb. If ignored, this significantly impairs the operator's ability to see and work safely in low-light conditions, increasing the risk of collision or operational errors. Continued operation with inadequate lighting can lead to safety violations and accidents.

Installation Tip

Ensure the electrical connector is clean and properly seated to prevent intermittent operation or electrical issues. Avoid touching the glass or plastic lens of the new bulb with bare fingers, as oils can cause premature failure.

Service Note

Inspect the condition of the surrounding body panels and other lighting components, such as turn signals and work lights, when replacing a headlight assembly.
