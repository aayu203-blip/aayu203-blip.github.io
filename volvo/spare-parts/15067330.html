Failure Symptoms

The first indication of a failing steel disc is often clutch slippage, where engine RPMs increase without a corresponding increase in vehicle speed, especially under load. If ignored, this can progress to difficulty engaging gears or a complete inability to transmit power, leaving the vehicle immobile. The risk is significant damage to the transmission or flywheel due to excessive heat and friction.

Installation Tip

Ensure the flywheel surface is clean and free of oil or debris before installing the new disc. Properly align the disc with the pilot bearing to prevent damage during clutch assembly.

Service Note

When replacing the steel disc, always inspect the flywheel for hot spots or warping, and check the condition of the clutch release bearing and pressure plate.
