Failure Symptoms

The initial symptom of a failing ring seal is typically a visible hydraulic fluid leak from the affected component, such as a cylinder or valve body. If the leak is ignored, it can lead to a gradual loss of hydraulic pressure and performance, making the equipment sluggish or unable to complete its intended functions. The ultimate consequence is potential damage to hydraulic pumps and other components due to low fluid levels or contamination.

Installation Tip

Ensure the sealing surface is perfectly clean and free of any burrs or sharp edges before installing the new ring seal. Lubricate the seal lightly with the appropriate hydraulic fluid to prevent damage during installation.

Service Note

When replacing a ring seal, it is essential to inspect the mating surface for scratches or wear and to flush the hydraulic system if contamination is suspected.
