Failure Symptoms

Early signs of a failing distributor include intermittent engine hesitation or stumbling under acceleration. If ignored, the engine may develop persistent misfires, a rough idle, and a significant loss of power, making the equipment difficult to operate. Continued use with a faulty distributor can lead to increased fuel consumption and potential damage to the catalytic converter.

Installation Tip

Before removing the old distributor, carefully mark its position relative to the engine block to ensure correct initial timing alignment. Do not force the distributor into its bore; if it doesn't seat easily, recheck the alignment marks and ensure no debris is present.

Service Note

Inspect the ignition coil, spark plug wires, and spark plugs for signs of wear or damage when replacing the distributor.
