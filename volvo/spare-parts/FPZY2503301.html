Failure Symptoms

The most common symptom is the engine cooling fan not operating, or running at full speed when it shouldn't be. If unaddressed, this can lead to the engine temperature rising rapidly, especially under load or in warm weather. Persistent overheating can cause severe engine damage, including warped cylinder heads or blown head gaskets.

Installation Tip

Before disconnecting the old module, ensure the ignition is off and the battery is disconnected to prevent electrical shorts. Make sure the electrical connector clicks securely into place on the new module.

Service Note

When replacing the fan control module, it is recommended to check the condition of the cooling fan motor and the coolant level.
