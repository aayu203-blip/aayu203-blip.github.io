Failure Symptoms

A failing shut-off solenoid may prevent the engine from stopping when the key is turned off, or cause the engine to crank but not start due to a lack of fuel. If the solenoid fails in the 'off' position, the engine will not receive fuel and will not run. Continued operation with a malfunctioning solenoid can lead to issues with engine control and potentially prevent necessary shutdowns.

Installation Tip

Ensure the engine is completely stopped and the battery is disconnected before attempting to remove or install the solenoid. Avoid overtightening the solenoid into its housing, as this can damage the threads or the solenoid body.

Service Note

Check the fuel filter and fuel lines for any restrictions or leaks when replacing the shut-off solenoid.
