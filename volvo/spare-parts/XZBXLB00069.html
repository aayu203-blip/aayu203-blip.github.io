Failure Symptoms

The initial symptom of a failing travel motor seal kit is often a gradual loss of drive speed or power in one or both tracks. If the leak progresses, the machine may exhibit jerky movements, and significant hydraulic fluid loss can occur, potentially leading to pump damage. Continued operation with compromised seals risks internal damage to the travel motor itself.

Installation Tip

Carefully clean all sealing surfaces and grooves before installing the new seals to ensure a proper fit and prevent damage. Avoid using sharp tools that could nick or scratch the seal lips during installation.

Service Note

Inspect the travel motor shaft, housing, and hydraulic lines for wear or damage when replacing the seal kit.
