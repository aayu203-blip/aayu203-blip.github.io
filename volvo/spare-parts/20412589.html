Failure Symptoms

The initial symptom of a failing filler cap is often visible fluid leakage around the reservoir opening. If ignored, contaminants can enter the system, leading to increased wear on pumps and valves. The worst consequence is accelerated internal component damage due to abrasive wear from ingested dirt.

Installation Tip

Ensure the sealing surface on the reservoir is clean and free of debris before installing the new cap. Do not overtighten the cap, as this can damage the threads or the seal, compromising its effectiveness.

Service Note

When replacing the filler cap, check the fluid level and condition, and inspect the reservoir for any signs of internal damage or contamination.
