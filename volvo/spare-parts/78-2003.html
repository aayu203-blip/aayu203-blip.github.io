Failure Symptoms

The first sign of a problem is often a loose fit or rattling sound from the mirror arm, especially at higher speeds. If the cover is cracked or missing, the mirror assembly's internal wiring and mechanisms are exposed to dirt, moisture, and potential damage. This can lead to electrical issues, reduced mirror adjustment functionality, or premature failure of the mirror components.

Installation Tip

Ensure all mounting clips or fasteners are properly engaged before applying pressure to seat the cover fully. Avoid using excessive force, which could crack the plastic or damage the mirror housing.

Service Note

Inspect the mirror glass, adjustment motor, and heating element for any signs of damage or malfunction when replacing the arm cover.
