Failure Symptoms

A failing cab or body part might first present as unusual noises, such as rattling or squeaking, from the affected area. Over time, this can lead to visible damage like cracks or deformation of panels. If left unaddressed, it could compromise the overall structural integrity or safety of the vehicle's cabin.

Installation Tip

Before installation, ensure all mounting surfaces and hardware are clean and free from corrosion or damage. Be careful not to overtighten fasteners, which can strip threads or crack the component.

Service Note

Inspect adjacent body panels and mounting hardware for any signs of stress, wear, or corrosion during replacement.
