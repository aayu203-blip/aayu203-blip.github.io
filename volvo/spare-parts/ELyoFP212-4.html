Failure Symptoms

The first sign of a failing wiper motor is often slow or erratic wiper movement. If the problem persists, the wipers may stop completely or only operate intermittently. Continued operation without functional wipers significantly impairs operator visibility, increasing the risk of accidents.

Installation Tip

Ensure the electrical connector is clean and securely seated to prevent intermittent operation or electrical shorts. Do not force the motor onto the wiper linkage spline; it should slide on with moderate pressure.

Service Note

Inspect the wiper linkage and arms for wear or damage, and lubricate pivot points when replacing the wiper motor.
