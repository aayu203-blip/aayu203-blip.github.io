Failure Symptoms

The initial indication of a failing cab spoiler might be increased wind noise at highway speeds or a visible crack or misalignment. If left unaddressed, aerodynamic disturbances can lead to reduced vehicle stability and increased fuel consumption. Significant damage or detachment of the spoiler poses a risk of further aerodynamic issues and potential damage to adjacent body panels.

Installation Tip

Ensure all mounting points and surfaces are clean and dry before attaching the spoiler to prevent adhesion issues. Avoid applying excessive force during installation, as this can damage the mounting brackets or the spoiler itself.

Service Note

Inspect the cab seals and adjacent body panels for any signs of wear or damage when replacing the cab spoiler.
