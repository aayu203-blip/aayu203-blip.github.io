Failure Symptoms

A failing master cylinder often presents as a soft or spongy brake pedal that returns slowly to its original position. If left unaddressed, braking performance will significantly degrade, requiring greater pedal effort for reduced stopping power. This increases the risk of extended stopping distances and potential accidents.

Installation Tip

Ensure the brake fluid reservoir is filled to the correct level before bleeding the system. Never reuse old brake fluid, as it can contain contaminants that damage the new cylinder.

Service Note

Always bleed the brake system thoroughly after replacing the master cylinder to ensure proper brake function.
