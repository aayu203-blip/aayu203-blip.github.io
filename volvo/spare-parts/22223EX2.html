Failure Symptoms

A compromised lower step may present as looseness, excessive flexing, or a noticeable rattle during vehicle operation. Continued use of a damaged step increases the likelihood of a sudden failure or a slip hazard for personnel. This can lead to injuries and downtime for the vehicle.

Installation Tip

Ensure all mounting points are clean and free of rust or debris before attaching the new step for a secure fit. Do not reuse old mounting hardware if it shows signs of corrosion or damage.

Service Note

Inspect the adjacent body panels and mounting brackets for any signs of corrosion or damage when replacing a cab step.
