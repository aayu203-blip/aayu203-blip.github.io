Failure Symptoms

The first indication of a failing rear cam expansion plug is often a small, localized leak of oil or coolant from the rear of the engine. If left unaddressed, the leak can worsen, leading to significant fluid loss and potential overheating or lubrication issues. Continued operation with leaking plugs can compromise engine integrity.

Installation Tip

Thoroughly clean the plug bore in the engine block or head to ensure a proper seal, removing any rust or old sealant. Avoid using excessive force when driving the plug in, as this can distort the plug or damage the bore.

Service Note

When replacing expansion plugs, inspect the surrounding engine block and cylinder head for any signs of corrosion or cracking.
