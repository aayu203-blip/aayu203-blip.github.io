Failure Symptoms

A loose or failing flange screw may initially cause slight vibrations or unusual engine noises. If ignored, the connection can loosen further, leading to oil or coolant leaks, or potential damage to the mating components. The worst consequence is component detachment, which can cause severe engine damage and operational failure.

Installation Tip

Ensure the threads in both the screw and the receiving hole are clean and free of debris before installation. Do not reuse old or damaged screws, as they may not provide adequate clamping force.

Service Note

Inspect the mating surface for flatness and cleanliness, and check the condition of any gaskets or seals being compressed by the flange.
