Failure Symptoms

Visible cracks, significant deformation, or a loose mounting are the primary indicators of a compromised bumper. Continued operation with a damaged bumper leaves the vehicle's front-end components vulnerable to further damage from minor impacts. The worst consequence is reduced protection for essential systems like the radiator and headlights in the event of a more significant collision.

Installation Tip

Ensure all mounting brackets and fasteners are clean and free of corrosion before attaching the new bumper. Do not overtighten mounting bolts, as this can crack the plastic or strip the threads.

Service Note

Inspect the mounting brackets, headlights, and grille for any damage when replacing the bumper.
