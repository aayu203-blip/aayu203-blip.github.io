Failure Symptoms

The primary symptom of a faulty A/C control panel is the inability to adjust cabin temperature or fan speed, or the system not responding to button presses. If left unaddressed, the operator will be unable to regulate the cabin environment, leading to discomfort, reduced driver focus, and potential heat stress in warm conditions. In cold weather, the inability to defrost or defog windows can create a safety hazard.

Installation Tip

Before disconnecting the old panel, ensure the vehicle's ignition is turned off to prevent electrical surges. When connecting the new panel, ensure all electrical connectors are securely seated to avoid intermittent operation.

Service Note

Check the A/C system's refrigerant charge and compressor operation when troubleshooting or replacing the control panel.
