Failure Symptoms

Initial signs of an ECU failure may include intermittent engine performance issues, such as stalling or rough idling, and illuminated dashboard warning lights. If the problem progresses, the machine may experience a significant loss of power or fail to start altogether. The worst-case scenario is a complete loss of machine control, rendering the equipment inoperable and potentially causing damage if critical functions are suddenly disabled.

Installation Tip

Before removing the old ECU, disconnect the battery and ensure all electrical connectors are clean and free from corrosion. Improperly seated connectors after installation can lead to communication errors or prevent the unit from functioning.

Service Note

After installing a new ECU, it is crucial to perform a diagnostic scan to clear any old fault codes and calibrate the unit to the machine's specific parameters.
