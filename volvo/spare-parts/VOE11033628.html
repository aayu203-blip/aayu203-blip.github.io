Failure Symptoms

The first sign of a failing heat exchanger is often a gradual increase in engine operating temperature, especially under load. If left unaddressed, this can lead to severe overheating, causing engine damage and potential shutdown. Continued operation risks significant damage to the engine block and cylinder head.

Installation Tip

Thoroughly flush the cooling system before installing the new heat exchanger to remove any debris that could cause premature blockage. Ensure all hose connections are securely clamped to prevent coolant leaks.

Service Note

Inspect and clean the radiator fins, check all coolant hoses for cracks or leaks, and verify the operation of the cooling fan when replacing the heat exchanger.
