446-002GLASS Volvo Fl / Fe Headlamp Glass Lh — Volvo

This headlamp glass is the protective lens for the left-hand headlight assembly on Volvo FL and FE series trucks. It is manufactured to meet Volvo's optical and impact resistance standards, ensuring clear illumination and durability over a worn or cracked original lens. Symptoms of a damaged glass include reduced light output, moisture ingress into the lamp housing, and potential for the lens to shatter further.

Failure Symptoms

A cracked or clouded headlamp glass will reduce the effectiveness of your headlights, making nighttime driving more hazardous. If moisture enters the housing, it can cause corrosion on electrical connections and premature bulb failure. Continued use with a compromised lens can lead to the glass shattering completely, potentially causing damage to other front-end components.

Installation Tip

Carefully clean the headlight housing mating surface to ensure a good seal with the new glass. Avoid overtightening the retaining clips or screws, which can crack the new lens.

Service Note

Inspect the headlight bulb and reflector for signs of corrosion or damage, and check the condition of the headlight housing seals.
