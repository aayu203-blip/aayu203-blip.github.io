Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping to Botswana take?
Air freight (DHL/FedEx) from Mumbai to Botswana typically takes 5–7 business days. Sea freight takes 14–28 days and is suitable for large bulk orders. All orders confirmed before 3 PM IST ship the same day.
Do you supply both OEM and aftermarket Scania parts?
Yes — we supply OEM-specification aftermarket Scania parts as standard, and can source genuine OEM parts on request. Both grades ship with commercial invoice, packing list, and certificate of origin.
What documentation do you provide for international orders?
Every export shipment includes a commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin (COO), and GST invoice. HS codes are declared accurately on all documents. LC (Letter of Credit) is accepted for large orders.
Can you source Scania parts not listed on your website?
Yes — send us the part number on WhatsApp and we will check availability within 60 minutes from our network of 200+ suppliers across India. Hard-to-find and discontinued parts are a speciality.
What payment methods do you accept?
Wire transfer (TT/SWIFT), Letter of Credit (LC for orders above USD 10,000), and online payment for smaller orders. We invoice in USD, EUR, or INR.
Do you supply Scania DC13 engine parts?
Yes — DC13A, DC13B, DC13C, and DC13-500/540 engine parts are all stocked. Key parts include XPI injectors (1876756), EGR coolers, EGR valves (1942552), VGT turbochargers, oil filters (1318046), and fuel filters (2010726).
Can you supply Scania Opticruise gearbox parts?
Yes. We stock GRS905R and GRS895R selector pistons (1779734), solenoid valves, air cylinders, and full pneumatic actuator kits. We also supply Scania manual gearbox parts for older GRS and GRSO units.
Do you stock Scania brake parts for the R series?
Yes — brake discs (1746278), brake pads, EBS modulators, brake chambers, slack adjusters, and wheel seal kits for the full P/R/G/S series front and drive axles.
