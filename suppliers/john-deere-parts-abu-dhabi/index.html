Yes — we supply OEM-specification aftermarket John Deere parts as standard, and can source genuine OEM parts on request. Both grades ship with commercial invoice, packing list, and certificate of origin.
What documentation do you provide for international orders?
Every export shipment includes a commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin (COO), and GST invoice. HS codes are declared accurately on all documents. LC (Letter of Credit) is accepted for large orders.
Can you source John Deere parts not listed on your website?
Yes — send us the part number on WhatsApp and we will check availability within 60 minutes from our network of 200+ suppliers across India. Hard-to-find and discontinued parts are a speciality.
What payment methods do you accept?
Wire transfer (TT/SWIFT), Letter of Credit (LC for orders above USD 10,000), and online payment for smaller orders. We invoice in USD, EUR, or INR.
Do you supply John Deere 210G excavator parts?
Yes — the 210G and 210GLC are our highest-volume John Deere excavator models. We stock 6068H engine filters, hydraulic pump seal kits (RE508472), cylinder seal kits, swing motor seals, and undercarriage components.
Can you supply John Deere PowerTech engine parts?
Yes — PowerTech 4045HT (310K, 130G), PowerTech 6068H (210G, 6R tractors), and PowerTech 6135HH (350G, 7R/8R tractors). Common parts: injectors, oil filters (RE504836), fuel filters (RE509672), water pumps, and gasket sets.
Do you supply John Deere tractor parts for the 6R series?
Yes. We stock engine filters, PowerShift transmission seal kits, hydraulic pump components, electrical parts, and cab components for the 6R110 through 6R250 range, which are widely used across South Asia and Africa.
